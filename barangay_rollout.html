Methodology & Framework
The Strategic Triad: Multi-Factor Scoring Engine
Slide 2 / 12

1. Necessity (30%)

Unserved FTTH Gap & Demand

FWA delivers high-speed wireless home broadband where pure wireline fiber drops cannot reach economically.

  • Rural (`R`) Classification: Dispersed houses & long drops make FTTH unviable for telcos.
  • Class 4–6 Municipalities: Neglected by major wireline builds; acute home broadband desert.
  • Exclusion of Metro Cores: Avoids dense FTTH-saturated areas (inner NCR).

2. Business Viability (35%)

Catchment Sweet Spot & Sizing

Radio capacity is dimensioned for 300 to 600 active subscribers per 3-sector site.

  • Golden Catchment: 3,000–12,000 population (~700–2,700 HH) easily fills 300–600 subs at 25-30% penetration.
  • Real Household Sizing: Uses municipality-specific HH multipliers (3.1 to 7.56).
  • Economic Corridors: Prioritizes high-remittance regions (CALABARZON, Visayas, Central Luzon).

3. Ease of Deployment (35%)

Converge Infrastructure Leverage

Backhaul is the #1 cost and timeline bottleneck. Leveraging existing Converge assets changes the game.

  • Tier 1A (3+ Nodes in Muni): Score 95–100. Immediate optical lateral connection (<1–3 km).
  • Tier 1B (1–2 Nodes in Muni): Score 85–92. Active node in municipality.
  • Zero Transit Penalty: Eliminates $1,800/mo satellite fees; cuts site turn-up to <30 days.

Why Focus on 1 Site per Top Barangay?

  • Maximum Geographic Footprint: 1,000 sites across 1,000 barangays reaches 271 municipalities in 46 provinces simultaneously.
  • Zero Self-Interference: Single macro site covers the entire poblacion or rural center without adjacent channel co-interference in early phases.
  • Rapid Market Preemption: Locks in the community before mobile operators or local WISPs can react.
  • Expansion Readiness: Denser barangays (>12k pop) can receive secondary split sectors in Batch 3 & 4.

Key Takeaway from Household Sizing Correction

Applying actual municipal household sizes (3.1 to 7.56) instead of a flat 4.4 divisor revealed critical operational realities:

  • High Household Density in Luzon: Cavite, Batangas, Laguna, Ilocos have smaller HH sizes (3.6–4.2), meaning more paying households per 1,000 population.
  • BARMM Household Reality: Sulu, Tawi-Tawi, Lanao have 5.8–7.5 people per household, resulting in fewer distinct subscription units per macro tower.

Implementation
Site Acquisition & Turn-Up Execution Playbook
Slide 11 / 12
Day 1 - 30

1. Site Acquisition

  • Submit target coordinates to TowerCos (EDOTCO, EdgePoint, PhilTower).
  • Prioritize existing towers within 1.5 km of barangay hall.
  • Secure municipal LGU clearance and barangay council resolutions.
Day 15 - 45

2. Converge OSP Link

  • Issue work order to Converge Outside Plant (OSP) engineering.
  • Splice optical drop cable from matching backbone node (0–3 km).
  • Terminate on dedicated 10G SFP+ port in baseband cabinet.
Day 30 - 60

3. RAN & Core Integration

  • Install 3x 5G Active Antenna Units (AAUs) on tower mount.
  • Power connection & battery backup installation.
  • Commission gNodeB with Comclark 5G Core & OSS/BSS.
Day 45 - 75

4. Sales & CPE Launch

  • Deploy barangay commercial roadshow & local load agents.
  • Offer zero-cashout CPE self-install kits for ₱599/mo promo.
  • Target 40% subscriber fill rate within first 45 days of on-air.
