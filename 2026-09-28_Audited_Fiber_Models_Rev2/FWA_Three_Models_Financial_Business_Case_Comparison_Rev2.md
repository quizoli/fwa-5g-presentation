### Key Financial Insights & Structural Audit Findings

1. **Model 1 (Commercial Anchor):**
   - Delivers exceptional cash returns driven by 100% Fiber-First deployment in Batch 1 (919 Class A + 81 Class B sites).
   - Recovers its cumulative capital within the 10-year modeling horizon (9.7-year simple payback, $336.1M peak cash deficit).

2. **Model 2 (Universal Access / GIDA):**
   - Due to low household density and challenging topography, operational revenues in pure GIDA areas do not cover standalone macro site leasing and active maintenance without government subsidy.
   - Cash flows remain negative across all 10 years, resulting in **"No Payback (>10 Yrs)"** and a cumulative deficit of $1,171.3M. This underscores the necessity of DICT/Universal Service Fund (USF) capital subsidies or Starlink bulk pooling for remote deployment.

3. **Model 3 (Recommended Combined Hybrid Plan):**
   - The optimal commercial strategy: high-margin commercial fiber sites (Batches 1 & 2) generate positive cash flows that cross-subsidize 2,000 remote GIDA sites.
   - Achieves project viability with **+$486.3M NPV**, **26.8% IRR**, an **8.3-year payback**, and a manageable **$202.2M peak funding requirement**.

4. **NTC Spectrum Users Fee (SUF) Statutory Basis (₱400M / $7.27M USD per year):**
   - **Spectrum Bandwidth:** 80 MHz contiguous channel in band n50 (1427–1518 MHz), corresponding to 80,000 kHz.
   - **Statutory Tariff:** Mandated NTC fee rate of **₱5.00 per kHz per station per annum**.
   - **Station Count:** Baseline network deployment footprint of 1,000 macro base stations.
   - **Total Annual Calculation:**  
     $$\text{SUF} = 80,000\text{ kHz} \times \text{₱}5.00/\text{kHz} \times 1,000\text{ stations} = \text{₱}400,000,000\text{ PHP/year} \quad (\approx \$7,272,727\text{ USD/year at ₱55/\$})$$
