### Strategic Commentary
* **Model 1 (Commercial):** Maximizes cash flow return and delivers a unicorn valuation (+$1.07B NPV) with rapid 4.8-year payback, driven by dense highway clustering and 98% fiber colocation.
* **Model 2 (GIDA):** Requires DICT/Universal Service Fund CAPEX subsidies or Starlink bulk pooling to offset the higher satellite OPEX in remote island communities.
* **Model 3 (Combined):** Delivers a robust **+$686.3M NPV (26.8% IRR)**, allowing high-margin commercial fiber sites to cross-subsidize 2,000 remote Starlink GIDA sites.
