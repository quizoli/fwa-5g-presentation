## 2. Key Takeaways from the Independent Fiber Audit

1. **Phase 1 Commercial is 90.9% Field-Ready:**  
   824 sites are within 1 km of a verified node candidate, and 85 more have a verified cable route within 500 meters. Mean node distance in Phase 1 is **0.67 km**. Only 2 sites in Phase 1 (Culasi, Roxas City and Nailon, Bogo City) are beyond 3 km.
2. **False-Positive Removal:**  
   Screened out 1,109 non-node placemarks (poles, handholes, slack loops, and "proposed" CO markers), ensuring engineering survey teams do not search for central offices that have not yet been constructed.
3. **Pragmatic Role of Starlink LEO Satellite:**  
   Terrestrial fiber cannot economically reach 100% of the Philippines. For Model 2 (GIDA), **66.5% of sites** are beyond 3 km from fiber, making Starlink LEO satellite backhaul an indispensable operational anchor.

## 3. Executive Recommendation for Leadership

* **Adopt Model 3 (Combined Hybrid) for Phase 1 Rollout:**  
  Model 3 provides the optimal compromise: capturing **761,219 addressable subscribers** in Phase 1 (generating immediate positive EBITDA), while dedicating **25% of initial build to remote GIDA communities** via Starlink satellite backhaul.
