The Philippine Rural & Suburban Broadband Deficit
Slide 04 / 20

Unaddressed Market Fundamentals

  • 27 Million Total Households across the Philippines; only 7.5 Million connected to FTTH.
  • 19.5 Million Unserved / Underserved Households (72%) relying on expensive, metered mobile data buckets (₱10–₱20/GB).
  • Incumbent Under-Investment: Major telcos (PLDT, Globe, Converge) concentrate FTTH rollout in NCR and Metro Cebu/Davao, avoiding Class 3–6 municipalities due to high civil fiber construction costs.
  • Strong Rural Disposable Income: Driven by OFW family remittances, agricultural logistics, and decentralized remote work.

Our Competitive Edge: Rapid FWA

  • Zero Trenching & Immediate Commissioning: A site with Starlink backhaul goes live in 48 hours, vs. 12–18 months for municipal fiber right-of-way.
  • Sub-1.5GHz Concrete Penetration: Band n50 signals easily pass through standard concrete and hollow-block home construction, avoiding expensive outdoor rooftop antenna installations.
  • Sub-₱600 Uncapped Value: Delivering 25–50 Mbps uncapped/generous quota at ₱599–₱699/mo provides unmatched customer value compared to mobile pocket Wi-Fi.
Target Municipal Profile (First 1,000 Sites):

Top 249 Class 3 to 6 municipalities in Luzon, Visayas, and Northern Mindanao with population densities of 300–800 households per km² and zero commercial FTTH competition.

Why Band n50 Beats 3.5 GHz (n78)

Mid-band 3.5 GHz signals suffer high atmospheric attenuation and penetrate concrete poorly, limiting cell radius to 1.2 km (requiring 8.5x more towers to cover the same geography).

1.5 GHz provides the perfect balance between massive spectral throughput and macro propagation reach.

Macro Hardware Configuration (Turnkey Price Book)

  • RAN Services: \$15,900 / site (Site survey, PM, I&C, RF optimization).
  • Customer Premises Equipment (CPE): \$55.00 / unit (Sub-1.5GHz 5G indoor gateway).

Facility Structure & Mechanics

  • Eligible Capital Assets: RAN BTS equipment ($26.0k/site), RAN turnkey services ($15.9k/site), Starlink hardware ($3.1k/site), and 5G Core/BSS hardware/licenses.
  • Down Payment: 20% cash payable on acceptance in the year of deployment.
  • Amortization Period: 80% financed balance repaid in 4 equal annual principal installments (20% p.a.).
  • Interest Cost: 6.5% per annum on the opening unpaid balance.

Strategic Balance Sheet Benefits

  • Eliminates Day-1 Dilution: Shields shareholders from aggressive initial equity calls when the subscriber base is in its ramp phase.
  • Self-Funding Debt Service: By Year 3, operational cash flow from the active subscriber base fully covers equipment debt service.
  • Optimized Corporate WACC: Incorporates low-cost equipment debt into the capital structure, lifting equity returns.
