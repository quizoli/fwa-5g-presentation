2. CLOUD-NATIVE 5G CORE (5GC SA) & DATA CENTER ARCHITECTURE (LOT 3)

2.1 User Plane Throughput Capacity Dimensioning

  1. Phase 1 Capacity Baseline (1,000,000 Subscribers):
    • The cloud-native 5G SA Core User Plane Function (UPF) cluster must be physically and logically dimensioned to deliver an initial aggregated user-plane throughput floor of 300 Gbit/s to 500 Gbit/s.
    • Dimensioning derives from an initial target of 300,000 concurrently active broadband households exhibiting an Average Busy-Hour Throughput (ABHT) of 1.5 Mbit/s to 2.0 Mbit/s per active subscriber at a standard 15% to 20% busy-hour concurrency factor.
  2. Expansion Horizon (3,000,000 Subscribers):
    • The proposed containerized 5G Core Network Functions (CNF) running on Kubernetes / bare-metal server infrastructure must natively support horizontal auto-scaling of distributed UPF instances up to an aggregated user-plane throughput capacity of 1.5 Tbit/s.
    • Expansion to 1.5 Tbit/s must be executable via software license expansion and incremental server node deployment without requiring architectural redesign, control plane migration (AMF/SMF/UDM), or database re-sharding.

3. RADIO ACCESS NETWORK (RAN), BAND n50 SPECTRUM & TRANSMISSION (LOTS 1 & 6)

3.1 Spectrum Physics, Operational Channel Window, RF Testing & Emission Suppression

  1. Active Operational Passband & Numerology:
    • Under the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) regulatory framework for dedicated broadband wireless communications, the authorized operational Band n50 carrier is established as 1457.0 MHz to 1517.0 MHz, providing a single contiguous 60.0 MHz TDD channel bandwidth centered at 1487.0 MHz (aligned on the standard 100 kHz 3GPP RF reference raster).
    • The carrier operates at 30 kHz Subcarrier Spacing (SCS, μ=1), dimensioned for 162 Physical Resource Blocks (PRBs) occupying 58.32 MHz active transmission bandwidth.
    • Spectral Efficiency Comparison: A single 60.0 MHz carrier provides 162 PRBs (97.2% frequency utilization), capturing +5.9% higher usable capacity and eliminating inter-carrier guard band waste and Carrier Aggregation (CA) overhead compared to 3x 20 MHz (153 PRBs total, 91.8% utilization).
  2. Guardband Allocation & Satellite Protection (> 1517.0 MHz):
    • A dedicated 1.0 MHz external guardband (1517.0 MHz to 1518.0 MHz) is allocated immediately adjacent to the upper band edge below the NTC allocation ceiling (1518.0 MHz).
    • Incorporating the internal 3GPP channel guardband of 840 kHz, the effective physical separation from the outermost active subcarrier (1516.16 MHz) to the sensitive satellite downlink boundary (1518.0 MHz) is 1.84 MHz.
    • All gNodeB transceivers must implement sharp digital filtering ensuring out-of-band emissions (OOBE) into the adjacent maritime mobile-satellite service band (1518.0 – 1525.0 MHz) do not exceed -30 dBm/MHz at the antenna connector, strictly compliant with ITU-R M.2159-0 / WRC-15 Resolution 223 and 3GPP TS 38.104 Category B.
  3. Lower Out-of-Band Emission Limits (1432.0–1457.0 MHz):
    • Radiated and conducted unwanted emissions in the adjacent 1432.0–1457.0 MHz block (allocated for Non-Public Networks / NPN under NTC MC 006-11-2025) must strictly meet 3GPP TS 38.104 Category B standards with an Adjacent Channel Leakage Ratio (ACLR ≥ 45 dB).
  4. In-Band Broadcasting-Satellite Service (BSS) Coexistence (1452.0–1492.0 MHz):
    • In accordance with Section IV of NTC MC No. 006-11-2025, gNodeB macro deployments overlapping the 1452.0–1492.0 MHz primary BSS allocation must support remote power control, electrical downtilt adjustments (eRET), and interference coordination to avoid detrimental interference to licensed satellite earth stations.
  5. Mandatory 3GPP TS 38.104 & TS 38.141 RF Conformance Testing Deliverables:
    • Shortlisted RAN bidders are formally required to submit certified third-party or OEM factory laboratory test reports under 3GPP TS 38.141-1 (Conducted) and TS 38.141-2 (Radiated) demonstrating:
      • Spectrum Emission Mask (SEM): Full compliance operating at maximum rated transmitter power (+55 dBm / 320 W conducted per 8T8R sector).
      • Adjacent Channel Leakage Ratio (ACLR): Measured at full load (162 PRBs active).
      • Operating Band Unwanted Emissions (OBUE): High-resolution spectral emission mask plots across the critical 1517.0 – 1559.0 MHz range (specifically proving the ≤ -30 dBm/MHz limit above 1518.0 MHz).
      • Spurious Emissions: Measured conducted unwanted emissions outside the operating band.
      • Power De-Rating & Filtering Declaration: If power back-off or an optional external high-Q cavity bandpass filter is required to satisfy emissions at the 1518.0 MHz boundary, the bidder must explicitly declare the exact de-rating (in dB) and quantify its impact on cell-edge link budget coverage.
  6. NTC Memorandum Circular No. 006-11-2025 & Spectrum Expansion Clarification:
    • Regulatory Allocation Framework: The National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) has promulgated NTC Memorandum Circular No. 006-11-2025 governing International Mobile Telecommunications (IMT) spectrum in the 1427.0–1518.0 MHz (1.5 GHz L-Band) frequency range:
      • 1427.0 – 1457.0 MHz (30 MHz): Allocated for Non-Public Networks (NPN) / dedicated private wireless networks.
      • 1457.0 – 1518.0 MHz (61 MHz): Allocated for Public Networks (PN), formally confirming the regulatory foundation for TelTech's authorized 1457.0–1517.0 MHz (60 MHz continuous TDD) active operational carrier.
    • Regulatory Consultations on Spectrum Expansion: The Operator is actively conducting regulatory and technical consultations with the NTC to clarify mechanisms for expanding operational spectrum bandwidth beyond the 60 MHz baseline (up to 80 MHz channel operation). Specifically, TelTech is clarifying whether supplemental operational access or secondary co-channel/adjacent-channel usage may be authorized, particularly across suburban, provincial, and rural clusters where:
      1. No Non-Public Networks (NPN) are licensed or physically operating within the 1427.0–1457.0 MHz block; and/or
      2. Incumbent mobile network operators (Globe Telecom, Smart Communications) are not actively utilizing the L-Band / Band n75 as Supplemental Downlink (SDL).
    • Technical Bidding Implications (Hardware & Software):
      • Hardware Wideband Native Capability: All proposed 5G NR Baseband Units (BBU), Remote Radio Units (RRU) / Active Antenna Units (AAU), and Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) RF front-ends must natively support the full 3GPP Band n50 tuning range (1432.0 – 1517.0 MHz, 85 MHz Instantaneous Bandwidth / IBW) in hardware.
      • Software Reconfigurability: Should the NTC grant supplemental operational bandwidth or regional secondary channels, systems must support software/firmware reconfiguration (e.g., expanding from 60 MHz to 80 MHz channel bandwidth or activating carrier aggregation) remotely without requiring physical antenna swaps, hardware replacements, or tower truck rolls.

6. OPERATIONAL SLAS, RADIO NETWORK KPIS & SSV ACCEPTANCE (APPENDIX C)

6.1 Radio Performance Indicators (Nationwide 24/7/366)

To harmonize rigorous carrier-grade service quality with real-world FWA wireless propagation environments, the following SLA thresholds are formally enacted:

  1. Call / Session Setup Success Rate (CSSR):
    • Target Standard: 97.0% (revised from 99.0%)
    • Minimum Threshold: 96.0% (revised from 97.5%)
    • Formula: $\text{CSSR} = \text{RRC Setup Success Rate} \times \text{5G QoS Flow Setup Success Rate} \times 100\%$
  2. Call Drop Rate (CDR):
    • Target Standard: $\le$ 1.5% (revised from $\le$ 0.5%)
    • Maximum Threshold: $\le$ 2.0% (revised from $\le$ 1.0%)
  3. Packet Loss Rate:
    • Evaluated at the PDCP (Packet Data Convergence Protocol) Layer pursuant to 3GPP TS 38.314.
    • Target Standard: $\le$ 0.10%; Maximum Threshold: $\le$ 0.20%.
  4. Time-Consistent Busy Hour (TCBH):
    • Evaluated on a per-cell basis as the rolling continuous 60-minute window demonstrating highest combined uplink/downlink traffic volume over a 30-day observation period.

6.2 Service Credit Assessment & Stabilization Governance

  1. Financial Penalty Floor:
    • Service Credit liquidated damages are assessed exclusively when monthly performance breaches the Minimum Threshold floor (or exceeds the Maximum Threshold for CDR/Packet Loss). Performance falling between the Target Standard and Minimum Threshold mandates engineering root-cause analysis and corrective cluster optimization without financial penalty.
  2. Post-PAC Cluster Stabilization Window:
    • A 90-day stabilization grace period is granted for each site rollout cluster commencing from Provisional Acceptance (PAC), during which financial Service Credits for Network Availability (NAV) and MTTR are suspended while operational baselines and field spares logistics are stabilized.
  3. OSS Throughput Counters vs. Contractual SLAs:
    • Financial penalties shall not be assessed against instantaneous OSS cell-edge throughput counters due to dependencies on subscriber traffic distribution and concurrency. Cell-edge speed compliance is governed by Single Site Verification (SSV) and cluster drive acceptance.

6.3 Single Site Verification (SSV) Speed Criteria by RSRP Brackets

Single Site Verification stationary throughput benchmarks for a 60 MHz Band n50 channel under a standard 8:2 (DDDDDDDSUU) or 7:3 TDD frame configuration are established as follows:

  • Near Zone (RSRP $\ge$ -80 dBm): Downlink $\ge$ 200 Mbit/s | Uplink $\ge$ 25 Mbit/s
  • Mid Zone (-95 dBm $\le$ RSRP < -80 dBm): Downlink $\ge$ 100 Mbit/s | Uplink $\ge$ 10 Mbit/s
  • Cell Edge Zone (-105 dBm $\le$ RSRP < -95 dBm): Downlink $\ge$ 35 Mbit/s | Uplink $\ge$ 5 Mbit/s

(Note: A 5 Mbit/s uplink floor is mandatory to guarantee TCP ACK throughput and interactive voice/video responsiveness).

6.4 Field Installation & Maintenance Metrics

  1. Subscriber Installation Scope:
    • Encompasses on-site RF signal verification, optimal wall/roof/pole mounting, outdoor weather-sealed Cat6 cabling (up to 30m), PoE injection, indoor Wi-Fi router setup, zero-touch provisioning via FMS app, and verified speed test sign-off.
  2. First-Time-Right (FTR) Installation Rate ($\ge$ 95%):
    • Calculated monthly as:

$$\text{FTR Rate} = \left( \frac{N_{\text{completed installations}} - N_{\text{repeat visits within 14 days}}}{N_{\text{completed installations}}} \right) \times 100\%$$

  • Any physical revisit required within fourteen (14) calendar days following sign-off due to workmanship defects, misaligned antennas, or provisioning errors is classified as a failed FTR event.
  1. Spare Parts Management Service (SPMS) NBD Delivery:
    • Next Business Day (NBD) replacement requires dispatch and physical delivery of replacement spare modules to the designated regional forward maintenance depot / TelTech regional maintenance center within the next business day (Monday through Saturday, cut-off 15:00) following RMA notification.
