## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW & SITING ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 Program Scale & Rollout Cadence
The TelTech 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) network delivers high-capacity broadband utilizing 3GPP Band n50 with an authorized operational passband of 1457.0–1517.0 MHz (60 MHz continuous TDD operating bandwidth) and wideband hardware tuning capability across 1432.0–1517.0 MHz (85 MHz IBW capability; software-configurable 40/50/60/80 MHz TDD) across 5,000 macro sites nationwide:
* **Phase 1 (Committed Network Baseline):** 1,000 Macro BTS Sites delivered within 365 calendar days.
* **Phases 2 to 5 (Contractual Expansion Batches):** 1,000 Sites per annum, scaling to 5,000 sites over 5 years.
* **Target Subscriber Capacity:** 365,000 active concurrent residential and SME subscribers (365 subs/site average).
* **Audited TowerCo Infrastructure:** 13,810 audited nationwide TowerCo registry records encompassing 13,799 unique Universal Tower IDs and 13,629 distinct coordinates across the Philippines.

### 1.2 Mandatory 3-Tier Sourcing Hierarchy
To achieve rapid market entry and minimize civil capex, infrastructure sourcing is strictly sequenced:
1. **Tier 1 (Mandatory Baseline - 89.7% / 897 Sites Phase 1):** Co-location on existing operational towers (PTCI/MIDC, EdgePoint, FTAP Globe, FTAP Smart, LDIC) within 500m (urban) or 1,000m (rural) of nominal search ring centroids.
2. **Tier 2 (Secondary Target - Nominal 103 Sites):** TowerCo Build-to-Suit (B2S) towers delivered turnkey by TowerCos under MLA where existing structures lack load headroom.
3. **Tier 3 (Contingency Standby Exception - 10.3% / 103 Sites):** Operator Greenfield Monopole Own-Build (80x 30m / 23x 40m) deployed strictly where zero TowerCos operate or all TowerCos issue formal structural denial letters.

## 2. DETAILED TECHNICAL SCOPE OF WORK (DEPLOYMENT & ROLLOUT SERVICES)

### 2.1 Scope 1: Site Acquisition (SAQ) & Legal Permitting Services (Standby Contingency Framework — Tier 3 Own-Build)
*Applicable strictly to Tier 3 Operator Own-Build sites authorized via formal written Work Order. Evaluated in Document 06 against the 103-site Phase 1 / 515-site Program contingency basket.*
* **Nominal Search Ring Siting & Candidate Hunting:** Field screening of 3 viable candidates (A/B/C) per search ring (500m urban / 1,000m rural), spatial screening against audited tower database (13,810 registry records / 13,799 unique IDs / 13,629 coordinates), and issuance of Preliminary Candidate Evaluation Report (PCER).
* **Site Acquisition Report (SAR) & Dossier:** Cadastral boundary audit, precision WGS84 GPS (>= 6 decimal places), land title verification (TCT/OCT/Tax Dec), lessor profile, and 360-degree panoramic photos.
* **TowerCo SAA Support:** Loading profile compilation (3x 8T8R RRU, 3x Antennas, hybrid cables, 2m x 2m pad) and technical coordination until SAA approval.
* **Leasing & Legal Notarization:** Securing 10-year registered lease contracts with right-of-way easements.
* **Statutory Permitting:** Barangay Resolution, LGU Building Permits under PD 1096, CAAP Height Clearance, DENR Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC/CNC), CFEI, FSIC, and Certificate of Occupancy.
* **Utility Interconnection:** Grid energization application with Meralco/Electric Cooperatives, transformer drop, and meter installation.

### 2.2 Scope 2: Site Engineering & Detailed Technical Site Survey (TSSR)
The Contractor shall conduct comprehensive multidisciplinary engineering site surveys:
* **Technical Site Survey (TSS):** Calibrated drone 360-degree panoramic photographic sweep at antenna centerlines (30m to 45m AGL); compound space audit (2.0m x 2.0m pad); cable gantry routing; and logistics transport access route survey.
* **Structural Loading & Wind Stress Assessment (Ownership Boundary):**
  - **Tier 1 (Co-Location) & Tier 2 (B2S):** Under MLA, the TowerCo conducts formal 3D structural analysis, TIA-222-H wind simulation, foundation capacity verification, and provides the licensed PE wet-sealed Structural Analysis Report (SAR) (per Appendix D §1.3 and Bid Bulletin 01 Huawei Item 13). Contractor scope is strictly physical audit of existing mounts, active equipment loading profile calculation (weight, EPA, mounting dimensions for 3x 8T8R RRU, 3x macro antennas/AAUs, AMB brackets, hybrid trunk cable), and mounting interface verification.
  - **Tier 3 (Operator Own-Build):** Contractor delivers turnkey 3D finite-element structural wind loading simulation under TIA-222-H and NSCP 2015 based on the site-specific basic wind speed ranging from **250 km/h (Zone III) to 315 km/h (Zone I typhoon corridors)** with Category 5 typhoon resilience, foundation verification, and certified SAR wet-sealed by a licensed Philippine Professional Civil/Structural Engineer.
* **Structural Engineering & Permitting Responsibility Matrix:**

* **Electrical & DC Power Assessment:** Utility transformer kVA audit, service entrance breaker check, DC power plant capacity check, and battery autonomy calculation (>= 4.0h @ 3.0 kW load, <= 80% DoD).
* **Earth Grounding Assessment:** Precision 3-point fall-of-potential test measuring earth resistance, mandating <= 5.0 Ohms pursuant to PEC and TelTech specifications.
* **RF Siting & Clutter Assessment:** Physical line-of-sight (LOS) inspection over target residential settlement, clutter clearance check, and field verification of simulated RSRP (>= -95 dBm cell edge).
* **Detailed Engineering Design (DED):** Complete multi-sheet AutoCAD DED blueprint dossiers signed and sealed by licensed Philippine Professional Engineers (PE Electrical, PE Mechanical, PE Civil/Structural).
* **As-Built Documentation:** Final post-construction redline CAD drawings and 50+ high-res photographic completion dossier.

### 2.3 Scope 3: Radio Network Planning & Design (RNPD)
* **Macro RF Propagation Modeling:** 3D ray-tracing propagation simulations calibrated against 5-meter clutter models and Digital Terrain Models (DTM); nominal search ring generation; and capacity dimensioning for Band n50 based on the **authorized 60 MHz continuous TDD baseline (1457.0–1517.0 MHz)** nationwide for contractual evaluation, with future software-expandable sensitivity modeling up to 80 MHz for provincial clusters pursuant to Bulletin No. 2 §3.1.
* **Cell Design Data (CDD) Engineering:** Parameter allocation for cell naming, gNodeB IDs, Physical Cell ID (PCI) planning adhering to modulo-3 and modulo-30 collision rules, Root Sequence Index (RSI), Tracking Area Codes (TAC), Neighbor Cell Lists (NCL / ANR), and PRACH parameters.

### 2.4 Scope 4: Telecom Logistics, Warehousing & Material Handling
* **Central & Regional Warehousing:** Bonded climate-controlled storage in central Mega Manila and regional hubs (North Luzon, South Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao); serial-number level barcode asset tagging; and ERP tracking.
* **BOM Kitting & Pre-Assembly:** Site kit staging (1x BBU, 3x 8T8R RRU, 3x macro antennas, patchcords, DC cables, SPDs, grounding accessories); pre-cutting cables; and firmware pre-flashing.
* **Regional Transport & Rough-Road Hauling:** Long-haul freight and final-mile 4x4 rough-road hauling, motorized bancas, or manual porterage to remote barangays, fully insured.

### 2.5 Scope 5: Telecom Installation, Testing, Commissioning & Integration (TI)
* **Tower Rigging & Antenna Installation:** Hoisting and mounting 3x macro antennas and 3x 8T8R RRUs on antenna mounting brackets (AMBs); setting azimuths (0°, 120°, 240°) and mechanical tilts; installing eRET AISG 2.0 cables.
* **Cabling & Feeder System:** Running armored optical jumpers and DC power cables along cable ladders; cold-shrink weatherproof boots; Type 2 SPDs at tower top and base; bonding grounding kits to busbars.
* **Baseband & Ancillary Installation:** 19" rack installation of BBU; GPS antenna installation; DC turn-up; software baseline loading.
* **Telecom Commissioning & Sweep Testing:** Optical power verification; VSWR sweep testing (VSWR <= 1.35:1, Return Loss >= 16.5 dB); PIM testing (<= -155 dBc).
* **Integration & On-Air Declaration:** IP backhaul routing integration; Core (UPF/AMF) connection; OSS synchronization; alarm clearance; and commercial on-air declaration.

### 2.6 Scope 6: Single Site Verification (SSV) & Site Acceptance (ATP)
Mandatory three-stage contractual acceptance procedure:
* **ATP-A (Mechanical, Civil & Electrical):** Joint visual inspection of bolt torque marks, cable dressing, weatherproofing, grounding resistance (<= 5.0 Ohms), and compound housekeeping.
* **ATP-B (Active Telecom & Functional):** Remote EMS verification of 8T8R MU-MIMO operation, remote eRET tilt control, and alarm failover simulations.
* **SSV (Single Site Verification):** Stationary throughput testing at near, mid, and cell-edge points per sector (validating >= 450 Mbps peak DL, >= 50 Mbps cell-edge DL, >= 15 Mbps cell-edge UL), sector swap checks, intra-site handover tests, and latency verification across backhaul media:
  - **Terrestrial Fiber Backhaul:** Target RTT **<= 18 ms** to Core UPF; Contractual Acceptance Rejection Ceiling **<= 30 ms** (per Appendix C KPI-RAN-07 and Bid Bulletin 01 §2.3). Sites measuring <= 30 ms meet provisional acceptance; sites > 30 ms require route re-engineering.
  - **LEO Satellite Backhaul (Starlink / Expansion Batches):** Target RTT **<= 60 ms**; Contractual Acceptance Rejection Ceiling **<= 80 ms** (strictly per Bid Bulletin 01 §2.3).

### 2.7 Scope 7: Cluster Radio Network Optimization (RNO)
Multi-site cluster optimization across contiguous clusters of 10–20 sites (65 clusters Phase 1 / 325 clusters Program): pre-launch drive testing, eRET optimization, azimuth adjustments, PCI re-planning, and 14-day post-launch OSS statistical validation confirming cluster Radio Availability >= 99.95%, Session Setup Success Rate (CSSR) >= 97.00% (min 96.00%), Session Drop Rate (CDDR) <= 1.50% (max 2.00%), and cluster average throughput >= 450 Mbps (harmonized w/ Bid Bulletin No. 2).

Mandated MTTR: **Severity 1 (Critical) <= 2.0h Urban / <= 4.0h Rural; Severity 2 (Major) <= 4.0h Urban / <= 6.0h Rural; Severity 3 (Minor) <= 12.0h Urban / <= 24.0h Rural.** Includes active hardware swapping, passive electrical triage, and emergency fiber backhaul splicing.

* **Continuous RNO & Interference Hunting:** Ongoing RF KPI surveillance, weekly Worst Performing Cell (WPC) triage, spectrum analyzer sweeps for external jamming/boosters, and PIM testing (<= -155 dBc).
* **Facility, Power & Software Maintenance:** Tower anti-corrosion painting, plinth crack repair, utility Meralco/Coop coordination, 250-hour generator servicing, maintenance window patch deployments (00:00-05:00), and weekly automated backups.
