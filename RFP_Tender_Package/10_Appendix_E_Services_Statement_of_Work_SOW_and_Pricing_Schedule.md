## 1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW & SITING ARCHITECTURE

### 1.1 Program Scale & Rollout Cadence
The 5G Fixed Wireless Access network utilizes 3GPP Band n50 (1432–1517 MHz, 60 MHz TDD) deployed across high-density suburban and rural barangays. The turnkey rollout encompasses:
* **Phase 1 Baseline:** 1,000 Macro Sites deployed within 365 calendar days.
* **Full Program Scope:** Up to 5,000 Macro Sites nationwide across Phases 2 through 5.
* **Mandatory Siting Sourcing Baseline:**
  * **Tier 1 (Mandatory Baseline - 89.7% / 897 Sites):** Co-location on existing operational towers (PTCI/MIDC, EdgePoint, FTAP Globe, FTAP Smart, LDIC) within the designated search ring.
  * **Tier 2 (Secondary Target):** TowerCo Build-to-Suit (B2S) where existing structures lack load capacity.
  * **Tier 3 (Restricted Exception - 10.3% / 103 Sites):** Direct Operator Greenfield Monopole construction (80x 30m / 23x 40m).

## 2. DETAILED TECHNICAL SCOPE OF WORK (DEPLOYMENT & ROLLOUT)

### 2.1 Scope 1: Site Acquisition (SAQ) & Legal Permitting Services
The Contractor shall deliver complete turnkey Site Acquisition (SAQ) and permitting services:
* **Candidate Hunting:** Siting and ranking three (3) viable candidates (Candidate A - Primary, Candidate B - Backup, Candidate C - Contingency) per nominal search ring (500m urban / 1,000m rural). Submission of the Preliminary Candidate Evaluation Report (PCER).
* **Site Acquisition Report (SAR):** Complete SAR package containing precision WGS84 GPS coordinates (6 decimals), ground elevation AMSL, cadastral property sketches, certified land title verification (TCT/OCT/Tax Declaration), lessor profile, power availability, 360-degree panoramic photos, and SAQ risk matrix.
* **TowerCo Space Application (SAA):** Compiling equipment loading profile (3x 8T8R RRU, 3x macro antennas/AAUs, AMB brackets, hybrid cables, 2m x 2m pad) and liaising with TowerCo engineering until issuance of SAA Approval and Notice to Proceed (NTP).
* **Greenfield Land Lease Support:** Lessor negotiations, title due diligence, long-term lease contract (10-15 years) drafting, and notarization.
* **Community & Statutory Permitting:** Securing Barangay Resolutions and Clearances, Homeowners Association (HOA) No Objection Resolutions, municipal LGU Building Permits (Architectural, Structural, Civil, Electrical, Mechanical) under PD 1096, CAAP Height Clearance Permits, DENR CNC/ECC, DPWH ROW clearances, Certificate of Final Electrical Inspection (CFEI), BFP Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC), commercial power utility energization (Meralco / Electric Cooperative), and LGU Certificates of Occupancy.

### 2.2 Scope 2: Site Engineering & Detailed Technical Site Survey (TSSR)
The Contractor shall conduct comprehensive multidisciplinary engineering site surveys:
* **Technical Site Survey (TSS):** 360-degree high-resolution panoramic photographic sweep at antenna centerlines (30m to 45m AGL) using calibrated drone flights; compound space audit (2.0m x 2.0m pad); cable gantry routing; and logistics route surveys.
* **Structural Loading & Wind Stress Assessment:** Comprehensive structural audit of steel members, gussets, and anchor bolts; calculation of total Effective Projected Area (EPA) and operating weight for 3x 8T8R RRUs, 3x macro antennas/AAUs, AMBs, and cables; wind stress simulation under TIA-222-H for 250 km/h basic wind speed (Category 5 typhoon resilience); foundation capacity verification; and formal endorsement by a licensed Professional Structural Engineer (PE).
* **Electrical & DC Power Assessment:** Utility transformer kVA audit, service entrance breaker check, DC power plant capacity check, and battery autonomy calculation (>= 4.0h @ 3.0 kW load, <= 80% DoD).
* **Earth Grounding Assessment:** Precision 3-point fall-of-potential test measuring earth resistance, mandating <= 5.0 Ohms pursuant to PEC and TelTech specifications.
* **RF Siting & Clutter Assessment:** Physical line-of-sight (LOS) inspection over target residential settlement, clutter clearance check, and field verification of simulated RSRP (>= -95 dBm cell edge).
* **Detailed Engineering Design (DED):** Complete set of AutoCAD drawings (Site Layout, Tower Elevation, AMB Details, Cable Ladder Details, Single Line Diagram, Grounding Ring Plan, BoQ) signed and sealed by licensed Professional Engineers.
* **As-Built Documentation:** Post-construction redline CAD drawings, photographic completion book (>= 50 high-res timestamped photos per site), and final PE endorsement.

### 2.3 Scope 3: Radio Network Planning & Design (RNPD)
The Contractor shall execute macro-level RF planning:
* **3D Ray-Tracing Propagation Modeling:** High-resolution 5-meter clutter and DTM simulation; nominal search ring generation; coverage prediction mapping (RSRP, SINR, Throughput); and capacity dimensioning for Band n50 (60 MHz TDD).
* **Cell Design Data (CDD) Engineering:** Cell naming taxonomy, gNodeB IDs, Physical Cell ID (PCI) planning adhering to modulo-3 and modulo-30 collision rules, Root Sequence Index (RSI), Tracking Area Code (TAC), Neighbor Cell Lists (NCL), PRACH parameters, transmit power allocation, and eRET tilt profiles.

### 2.4 Scope 4: Telecom Logistics, Warehousing & Material Handling
The Contractor shall provide complete logistics management:
* **Port Receiving & Central Warehousing:** Receiving OEM shipments at Port of Manila / Batangas; climate-controlled storage in Mega Manila and regional hubs (North Luzon, South Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao); serial-level barcode asset tagging; and ERP inventory tracking.
* **BOM Kitting & Pre-Assembly:** Picking and packing 1x BBU, 3x RRUs, 3x Antennas, optical/power cables, SPDs, grounding kits; cable pre-cutting based on approved TSSR; labeling; and factory firmware pre-flashing.
* **Regional Freight & Final-Mile Site Hauling:** Long-haul transport to regional hubs; dedicated final-mile rough-road delivery to remote barangay sites utilizing heavy-duty 4x4 trucks, motorized bancas, or manual porterage across unpaved terrain; comprehensive in-transit cargo insurance.

### 2.5 Scope 5: Telecom Installation, Testing, Commissioning & Integration (TI)
Rigorous execution by certified riggers and telecom engineers:
* **Rigging & Mounting:** Hoisting and mounting 3x macro high-gain antennas and 3x 8T8R RRUs on tower AMBs at designated AGL heights; setting mechanical tilts and azimuths (0°, 120°, 240°); installing eRET cables (AISG 2.0).
* **Cabling & Grounding:** Routing armored optical jumpers and DC power cables along cable ladders; installing cold-shrink weatherproof boots on all RF connections; installing Type 2 DC SPDs at tower top and base; bonding 3-point grounding kits to external ground busbar.
* **BBU Rack Wiring:** 19" rack installation of BBU; DC power wiring; backhaul transmission patchcords; GPS antenna installation for PTP/SyncE clock synchronization.
* **Commissioning & Sweep Testing:** DC power turn-up; software baseline loading; optical transceiver power verification; and calibrated RF sweep testing measuring Return Loss (>= 16.5 dB) and VSWR (<= 1.35:1) using a calibrated SiteMaster analyzer.
* **Network Integration & On-Air:** IP transmission turn-up (VLAN, BGP); S1-U / N2/N3 interface connection to 5G Core (AMF/UPF); synchronization with TelTech EMS/OSS; clearing all active alarms; and achieving commercial on-air status.

### 2.6 Scope 6: Single Site Verification (SSV) & Site Acceptance (ATP)
Mandatory three-stage acceptance procedure:
* **ATP-A (Mechanical, Civil & Electrical):** Joint visual inspection of bolt torque marks, cable dressing, weatherproofing, grounding resistance (<= 5.0 Ohms), and compound housekeeping.
* **ATP-B (Active Telecom & Functional):** Remote EMS verification of 8T8R MU-MIMO operation, remote eRET tilt control, and alarm failover simulations.
* **SSV (Single Site Verification):** Stationary throughput testing at near, mid, and cell-edge points per sector (validating >= 450 Mbps peak DL, >= 50 Mbps cell-edge DL, >= 15 Mbps cell-edge UL), sector swap checks, handover tests, latency verification (<= 18 ms RTT), and submission of the signed SSV Report.

### 2.7 Scope 7: Cluster Radio Network Optimization (RNO)
Multi-site cluster optimization across contiguous clusters of 10–20 sites:
* **Cluster Drive Testing:** Continuous scanner and test UE drive testing; capturing RSRP, SINR, throughput, and coverage overlap; identifying pilot pollution and coverage holes.
* **Physical & Soft Optimization:** Electrical downtilt (eRET) optimization; azimuth adjustments; PCI re-planning; neighbor list cleanup; handover parameter tuning.
* **Cluster Acceptance Sign-Off:** 14-day post-launch OSS statistical validation confirming cluster Radio Availability >= 99.95%, Session Setup Success Rate (CSSR) >= 99.60%, Session Drop Rate (CDDR) <= 0.15%, and cluster average throughput >= 450 Mbps.

## 3. OPERATIONS & MAINTENANCE (O&M) / MANAGED SERVICES SPECIFICATION

The Contractor shall deliver an integrated 24/7/365 Managed Services organization to ensure continuous network availability across all 5,000 sites nationwide:

### 3.1 24/7/365 Central NOC Surveillance & Incident Triage
* **Tier-1 Surveillance:** Continuous 24/7 active monitoring across RAN, Core, Power, and Transmission via EMS/OSS. Automated alarm correlation, deduplication, and suppression of transient alarms.
* **Incident Management & Ticketing:** Automated trouble ticket generation within 5 minutes of alarm onset. Incident triage, severity classification (Sev 1 Critical to Sev 4 Warning), and immediate dispatch.
* **Operational Reporting:** Daily Operational Flash Report at 06:00 PHT summarizing availability, outages, MTTR compliance, and open tickets, accompanied by weekly operational reviews and monthly SLA governance dossiers.

### 3.2 Tier-2 / Tier-3 Technical Support & OEM Vendor Escalation
* **Tier-2 Remote Diagnostics:** 24/7 remote engineering diagnostics, soft/hard resets, card re-initialization, backup configuration restorations, transmission routing failover, and software rollbacks.
* **Tier-3 OEM TAC Management:** Escalation for complex protocol bugs and crashes. Case logging with OEM TAC Level 3, trace capture, hotfix deployment, and formal Root Cause Analysis (RCA) reporting within 24 hours.

### 3.3 Field First Line Maintenance (FLM) & Emergency Corrective Restoration
* **Dedicated 24/7 Mobile Crews:** Strategically stationed nationwide in regional depots, equipped with 4x4 vehicles, safety rigging gear, calibrated test instruments, fusion splicers, OTDRs, and emergency spares.
* **Mandated MTTR SLAs:**
  * **Severity 1 (Critical - Site Outage / Major Core Failure):** Response <= 15 Mins | MTTR <= 2.0h (Urban) / <= 4.0h (Rural)
  * **Severity 2 (Major - Sector Down / Power Degraded):** Response <= 30 Mins | MTTR <= 4.0h (Urban) / <= 6.0h (Rural)
  * **Severity 3 (Minor - Non-Service Affecting Fault):** Response <= 1 Hour | MTTR <= 12.0h (Urban) / <= 24.0h (Rural)
  * **Severity 4 (Warning / Planned Maintenance):** Response <= 4 Hours | MTTR <= 48.0h (Urban) / <= 72.0h (Rural)
* **Active Hardware Swapping:** Fast-track replacement of faulty RRUs, BBUs, SFP transceivers, jumpers, and GPS antennas from regional spares.
* **Passive Power Restoration:** Resetting/replacing tripped DC breakers, rectifying rectifier faults, clearing battery BMS lockouts, and replacing blown SPDs.
* **Fiber Transmission Restoration:** Emergency OTDR fault localization, optical cable replacement, fusion splicing, and enclosure sealing.

### 3.4 Scheduled Routine Preventive Maintenance (PM) Program
Execution of 100% quarterly routine PM audits using the Operator's Mobile Field App with GPS-stamped, timestamped before-and-after photos:
* **Module A (Telecom Equipment - Quarterly):** Visual inspection of BBU/RRU chassis; optical fiber cleaning; RF jumper weatherproofing boot inspection; cable clamp torque check; optical SFP power level verification; and cabinet dust filter cleaning.
* **Module B (DC Power & LiFePO4 Battery - Quarterly):** Rectifier module load sharing verification; DC float/equalize voltage calibration; terminal screw torque checks; LiFePO4 battery BMS diagnostic sweep; cell voltage and internal resistance measurement; and 1-hour controlled battery discharge autonomy test under load.
* **Module C (Earth Grounding & Lightning Protection - Quarterly):** 3-point fall-of-potential ground resistance testing mandating <= 5.0 Ohms; Cadweld bond inspection; copper busbar torque check; corrosion audit; and air terminal alignment.
* **Module D (Tower Structure & Rigging - Bi-Annual):** Climbing audit of steel members, monopole climbing ladders, fall arrest cables, AMB mounting bracket bolt torques, antenna azimuth/tilt checks, cable gantry stability, and CAAP aviation light operation.
* **Module E (Compound Civil & Security - Quarterly):** Perimeter fence and barbed wire repair, gate lock lubrication, weed cutting and vegetation clearance within 3m perimeter, drainage clearance, and cabinet cable entry rodent/pest proofing.

### 3.5 Spare Parts Management & RMA Logistics (SPMS)
* **Regional Buffer Float:** Climate-controlled regional spare parts depots (Mega Manila, North Luzon, South Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao), maintaining an audited 2.0% active hardware spares float.
* **Advance Hardware Replacement (AHR):** Guaranteed Next-Business-Day (NBD) standard delivery nationwide, or expedited 4-hour emergency delivery for critical core and transmission hubs.
* **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA):** Defective unit collection, packaging, customs export/import clearance, OEM factory repair tracking (TAT <= 30 calendar days), and restocking.

### 3.6 Continuous Radio Network Optimization & Quality Assurance
* **Routine RF KPI Tracking:** Daily and weekly monitoring of Radio Availability (>= 99.95%), CSSR (>= 99.60%), CDDR (<= 0.15%), average throughput, and PRB utilization. Weekly analysis and remediation of Worst Performing Cells (WPC).
* **Interference Hunting & PIM Mitigation:** Spectrum analyzer sweeps to detect and eliminate external RF jamming, unauthorized cellular boosters, or microwave leakage; calibrated PIM testing and remediation on antenna lines; and field drive audits for high-churn clusters.

### 3.7 Passive Facility, Power Utility & Software Lifecycle Maintenance
* **Civil & Tower Facility Upkeep:** Structural steel rust scraping, cold galvanizing, and repainting in coastal/corrosive areas; concrete equipment plinth crack repair; and access road drainage clearance.
* **Utility Grid & Genset Coordination:** Commercial power utility (Meralco/Coop) meter reading audits and outage coordination; routine 250-hour preventive servicing on backup diesel generator sets; and emergency bulk fuel deliveries.
* **Software Support & Cybersecurity:** Applying OEM-approved firmware updates and security patches during maintenance windows (00:00–05:00); CVE vulnerability patching; and automated weekly BBU and Core configuration backups with secure offsite archiving.
