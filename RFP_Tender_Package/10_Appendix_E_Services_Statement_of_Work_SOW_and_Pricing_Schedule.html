EXPLICIT GOVERNANCE & COMMERCIAL RECONCILIATION PRINCIPLES

1. Primary Operational Baseline (TowerCo-Led Scope on 13,810 Audited Tower Locations): The primary operational objective of the Operator is to utilize Independent Tower Companies (TowerCos) under Master Lease Agreements (MLA) — either through Tier 1 (Existing Co-Location on the audited master database of 13,810 registry records / 13,799 unique IDs / 13,629 coordinates, 89.7% / 897 Sites) or Tier 2 (TowerCo Build-to-Suit / B2S, nominal 103 Sites). Under this primary baseline, all Site Acquisition (SAQ) work — including candidate hunting, landlord negotiations, title due diligence, lease execution, Barangay clearances, LGU building permits, CAAP clearances, and utility power applications — is STRICTLY THE SCOPE AND FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY OF THE TOWERCO, NOT THE VENDOR.

2. Structural Engineering & Permitting Ownership Boundary: Under MLA, for Tier 1 Co-Location and Tier 2 B2S, formal 3D structural analysis, TIA-222-H wind simulation (250–315 km/h site-specific basis), foundation capacity verification, and licensed Philippine Professional Civil/Structural Engineer (PE) wet-seal certification STRICTLY BELONG TO THE TOWERCO (per Appendix D §1.3 and Bid Bulletin 01 Huawei Item 13). Vendor scope is strictly limited to physical auditing, compiling active equipment loading specifications (weight, EPA, mounting brackets), and verifying the antenna mount interface. Vendor structural PE endorsement applies strictly to Tier 3 Operator Own-Build.

3. Standby SAQ Contingency Rate Card (10.3% Evaluation Basket): The unit rates requested under Category 1 (Items SAQ-01 to SAQ-08) serve STRICTLY AS A STANDBY / CONTINGENCY CALL-OFF RATE CARD ('Operator Self-Acquisition / Own-Build Option'). To allow fair commercial evaluation without inflating baseline contract commitments, SAQ lines are evaluated against an explicit Standby Contingency Evaluation Basket of 103 Sites (Phase 1) / 515 Sites (Program), representing the 10.3% Own-Build ratio. Billing is strictly triggered upon formal issuance of Operator Work Orders for authorized self-build sites. Bidders shall NOT embed SAQ risk premiums into baseline turnkey hardware or installation pricing.

4. Annualized & 3-Year Contractual O&M Evaluation (No Double-Counting): Recurring 24/7 Managed Services are evaluated under standardized multi-year periods: Monthly retainers × 12 months; Quarterly PMs × 4 quarters; Semi-Annual PMs × 2 semi-annuals. Category B defines both an Annualized 1-Year O&M Baseline and a 3-Year Managed O&M Contractual Baseline (36 Months) matching RFP Lot 6. Retainer-included items (FLM-02 to 04, SPM-02 to 03) evaluate at $0.00 to guarantee zero double-charging.

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW & SITING ARCHITECTURE

The 5G Fixed Wireless Access network utilizes 3GPP Band n50 with an authorized operational passband of 1457.0–1517.0 MHz (60 MHz continuous TDD operating bandwidth) and wideband hardware tuning capability across 1432.0–1517.0 MHz (85 MHz IBW capability; software-configurable 40/50/60/80 MHz TDD) deployed across high-density suburban and rural barangays. Siting delivery is strictly governed by the Mandatory 3-Tier Sourcing Hierarchy:

  • Tier 1 (Mandatory Baseline - 89.7% / 897 Sites Phase 1): Co-location on existing operational towers (PTCI/MIDC, EdgePoint, FTAP Globe, FTAP Smart, LDIC) within 500m (urban) or 1,000m (rural) of nominal search ring centroids across the audited master tower database (13,810 registry records / 13,799 unique IDs / 13,629 coordinates).
  • Tier 2 (Secondary Target - Nominal 103 Sites): TowerCo Build-to-Suit (B2S) where existing structures lack load capacity.
  • Tier 3 (Restricted Exception - 10.3% / 103 Sites): Direct Operator Greenfield Monopole construction (80x 30m / 23x 40m) deployed strictly where zero TowerCos operate or all TowerCos issue formal structural denial letters.

2. DETAILED TECHNICAL SCOPE OF WORK (DEPLOYMENT & ROLLOUT SERVICES)

2.1 Site Acquisition (SAQ) & Legal Permitting Services (Standby Contingency Framework — Tier 3 Own-Build)

Applicable strictly to Tier 3 Operator Own-Build sites authorized via formal written Work Order. Evaluated in Document 06 against the 103-site Phase 1 / 515-site Program contingency basket. Scope encompasses: hunting and ranking 3 viable candidates per search ring (500m urban / 1,000m rural); spatial screening against the 13,810 audited tower database; Preliminary Candidate Evaluation Report (PCER); Site Acquisition Report (SAR) with WGS84 GPS (6 decimals), cadastral sketches, clean title verification (TCT/OCT); TowerCo Space Application (SAA) loading dossier under MLA; greenfield lease negotiations; and statutory permits: Barangay Clearance, HOA Resolution, LGU Building Permits under PD 1096, CAAP Height Clearance, DENR ECC/CNC, CFEI, FSIC, and Certificate of Occupancy.

2.3 Radio Network Planning & Design (RNPD)

Macro-level 5G RF planning utilizing 3D ray-tracing propagation modeling engines calibrated against 5m clutter and DTM data; nominal search ring generation; coverage prediction mapping; capacity dimensioning for Band n50; and Cell Design Data (CDD) parameter engineering, including cell naming, gNodeB IDs, Physical Cell ID (PCI) planning with modulo-3/modulo-30 separation, Root Sequence Index (RSI), Neighbor Cell Lists (NCL), and eRET profiles.

2.4 Telecom Logistics, Warehousing & Material Handling

Central bonded climate-controlled warehousing in Mega Manila and regional hubs (North Luzon, South Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao); serial-number level barcode asset tagging; ERP tracking; staging site kits (1x BBU, 3x 8T8R RRUs, 3x macro antennas, optical patchcords, DC cables, SPDs, grounding accessories); pre-cutting cables; and long-haul and final-mile rough-road transport to remote barangay sites utilizing heavy-duty 4x4 trucks, motorized bancas, or manual porterage across unpaved terrain, fully insured.

2.5 Telecom Installation, Testing, Commissioning & Integration (TI)

Rigorous execution by certified riggers and telecom engineers: hoisting and mounting 3x macro high-gain antennas and 3x 8T8R RRUs on tower AMBs; setting mechanical tilts and azimuths (0°, 120°, 240°); installing eRET cables (AISG 2.0); routing armored optical jumpers and DC power cables along cable ladders; installing cold-shrink weatherproof boots; installing Type 2 DC SPDs at tower top and base; bonding 3-point grounding kits to external ground busbar; 19" rack installation of BBU; GPS antenna installation for PTP/SyncE; DC turn-up; software baseline loading; optical transceiver power verification; VSWR sweep testing (VSWR ≤ 1.35:1, Return Loss ≥ 16.5 dB); IP backhaul integration; Core (UPF/AMF) connection; OSS synchronization; alarm clearance; and commercial on-air declaration.

2.6 Single Site Verification (SSV) & Site Acceptance (ATP)

Mandatory three-stage acceptance procedure: ATP-A (Mechanical, Civil & Electrical inspection of bolt torque marks, cable dressing, weatherproofing, grounding resistance ≤ 5.0 Ohms); ATP-B (Active Telecom & Functional verification of 8T8R MU-MIMO operation, remote eRET tilt control, alarm failover simulations); and SSV (stationary throughput testing at near, mid, and cell-edge points per sector validating ≥ 450 Mbps peak DL, ≥ 50 Mbps cell-edge DL, ≥ 15 Mbps cell-edge UL, sector swap checks, handover tests, signed SSV Report, and latency verification):

  • Terrestrial Fiber Backhaul: Target RTT ≤ 18 ms to Core UPF; Contractual Acceptance Rejection Ceiling ≤ 30 ms (per Appendix C KPI-RAN-07 and Bid Bulletin 01 §2.3). Sites measuring ≤ 30 ms meet provisional acceptance; sites > 30 ms require route re-engineering.
  • LEO Satellite Backhaul (Starlink / Expansion Batches): Target RTT ≤ 60 ms; Contractual Acceptance Rejection Ceiling ≤ 80 ms (strictly per Bid Bulletin 01 §2.3).

2.7 Cluster Radio Network Optimization (RNO)

Multi-site cluster optimization across contiguous clusters of 10–20 sites (65 clusters Phase 1 / 325 clusters Program): pre-launch scanner and test UE drive testing; electrical downtilt (eRET) optimization; azimuth adjustments; PCI re-planning; neighbor list cleanup; handover parameter tuning; and 14-day post-launch OSS statistical validation confirming cluster Radio Availability ≥ 99.95%, Session Setup Success Rate (CSSR) ≥ 97.00% (min 96.00%), Session Drop Rate (CDDR) ≤ 1.50% (max 2.00%), and cluster average throughput ≥ 450 Mbps (harmonized w/ Bid Bulletin No. 2).

3. OPERATIONS & MAINTENANCE (O&M) / MANAGED SERVICES SPECIFICATION

The Contractor shall deliver an integrated 24/7/365 Managed Services organization to ensure continuous 99.95% network availability across all 5,000 sites nationwide:

  • 24/7/365 Central NOC Surveillance: Tier-1 real-time surveillance across RAN, Core, Power, and Transmission via EMS/OSS. Automated alarm correlation, deduplication, and trouble ticket creation within 5 minutes. Daily Operational Flash Reports at 06:00 PHT, weekly reviews, and monthly SLA dossiers.
  • Tier-2 / Tier-3 Support: 24/7 remote diagnostics, soft/hard resets, config restoration, and transmission failover. Tier-3 OEM TAC Level 3 escalation, bug patch testing, and Root Cause Analysis (RCA) reporting within 24 hours.
  • Field First Line Maintenance (FLM): Dedicated 24/7 mobile crews in 4x4 vehicles stationed at regional depots. Mandated MTTR: Severity 1 (Critical) ≤ 2.0h Urban / ≤ 4.0h Rural; Severity 2 (Major) ≤ 4.0h Urban / ≤ 6.0h Rural; Severity 3 (Minor) ≤ 12.0h Urban / ≤ 24.0h Rural. Includes active hardware swapping, passive electrical triage, and emergency fiber backhaul splicing.
  • Quarterly Preventive Maintenance (PM): 100% completion p.a. logged via Mobile App: Module A (Telecom Equipment & Optics); Module B (DC Power Plant & LiFePO4 Battery discharge test); Module C (Grounding grid ≤ 5.0 Ohms); Module D (Bi-annual tower climbing & rigging audit); Module E (Compound civil, fencing & weed clearance).
  • Spare Parts Management (SPMS): Regional depots maintaining an audited 2.0% active hardware float. Guaranteed Advance Hardware Replacement (AHR) Next-Business-Day (NBD) standard or 4-hour emergency delivery. Full RMA reverse logistics lifecycle tracking (TAT ≤ 30 days).
  • Continuous RNO & Interference Hunting: Ongoing RF KPI surveillance, weekly Worst Performing Cell (WPC) triage, spectrum analyzer sweeps for external jamming/boosters, and PIM testing (≤ -155 dBc).
  • Facility, Power & Software Maintenance: Tower anti-corrosion painting, plinth crack repair, utility Meralco/Coop coordination, 250-hour generator servicing, maintenance window patch deployments (00:00-05:00), and weekly automated backups.
