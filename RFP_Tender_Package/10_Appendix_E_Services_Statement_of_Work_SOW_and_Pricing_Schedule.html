1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW & SITING ARCHITECTURE

The 5G Fixed Wireless Access network utilizes 3GPP Band n50 (1432–1517 MHz, 60 MHz TDD) deployed across high-density suburban and rural barangays. Siting delivery is strictly governed by the Mandatory 3-Tier Sourcing Hierarchy:

  • Tier 1 (Mandatory Baseline - 89.7% / 897 Sites): Co-location on existing operational towers (PTCI/MIDC, EdgePoint, FTAP Globe, FTAP Smart, LDIC) within the designated search ring.
  • Tier 2 (Secondary Target): TowerCo Build-to-Suit (B2S) where existing structures lack load capacity.
  • Tier 3 (Restricted Exception - 10.3% / 103 Sites): Direct Operator Greenfield Monopole construction (80x 30m / 23x 40m).

2. DETAILED TECHNICAL SCOPE OF WORK (DEPLOYMENT & ROLLOUT SERVICES)

2.1 Site Acquisition (SAQ) & Legal Permitting Services

The Contractor shall deliver complete turnkey Site Acquisition (SAQ) and permitting services: hunting and ranking three (3) viable candidates per search ring (500m urban / 1,000m rural); compiling the Preliminary Candidate Evaluation Report (PCER); producing the comprehensive Site Acquisition Report (SAR) with WGS84 GPS (6 decimals), cadastral sketches, clean title verification (TCT/OCT), and 360-degree panoramic photos; preparing the TowerCo Space Application (SAA) loading dossier under the MLA; supporting greenfield lease negotiations; and securing all statutory permits: Barangay Clearance, HOA Resolution, LGU Building Permits under PD 1096, CAAP Height Clearance, DENR CNC/ECC, DPWH ROW clearance, CFEI, FSIC, power utility energization (Meralco/Coop), and LGU Certificate of Occupancy.

2.2 Site Engineering & Detailed Technical Site Survey (TSSR)

Rigorous multidisciplinary engineering surveys including: 360-degree drone panoramic photo sweeps at antenna centerlines (30m to 45m AGL); structural audit of steel members, gussets, and anchor bolts; calculation of Effective Projected Area (EPA) and operating weight for 3x 8T8R RRUs, 3x macro antennas, AMBs, and cables; wind stress simulation under TIA-222-H for 250 km/h basic wind speed (Category 5 typhoon); foundation verification; utility transformer kVA and DC power plant headroom audit; battery autonomy calculation (>= 4.0h @ 3.0 kW load); 3-point fall-of-potential ground resistance testing (mandating ≤ 5.0 Ohms); RF line-of-sight and clutter verification (RSRP ≥ -95 dBm); complete AutoCAD Detailed Engineering Design (DED) blueprints signed and sealed by licensed Professional Engineers; and post-construction As-Built CAD documentation.

2.3 Radio Network Planning & Design (RNPD)

Macro-level 5G RF planning utilizing 3D ray-tracing propagation modeling engines calibrated against 5m clutter and DTM data; nominal search ring generation; coverage prediction mapping; capacity dimensioning for Band n50 (60 MHz TDD); and Cell Design Data (CDD) parameter engineering, including cell naming, gNodeB IDs, Physical Cell ID (PCI) planning with modulo-3/modulo-30 separation, Root Sequence Index (RSI), Neighbor Cell Lists (NCL), and eRET profiles.

2.4 Telecom Logistics, Warehousing & Material Handling

End-to-end logistics management: port receiving at Manila/Batangas; bonded climate-controlled storage in Mega Manila and regional hubs (North Luzon, South Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao); serial-level barcode asset tagging in ERP; BOM kitting and cable pre-cutting based on approved TSSR; firmware pre-flashing; line-haul freight; and dedicated final-mile rough-road transport to remote barangay sites utilizing heavy-duty 4x4 trucks, motorized bancas, or manual porterage across unpaved terrain, fully insured.

2.5 Telecom Installation, Testing, Commissioning & Integration (TI)

Rigorous execution by certified riggers and telecom engineers: hoisting and mounting 3x macro high-gain antennas and 3x 8T8R RRUs on tower AMBs; setting mechanical tilts and azimuths (0°, 120°, 240°); installing eRET cables (AISG 2.0); routing armored optical jumpers and DC power cables along cable ladders; installing cold-shrink weatherproof boots; installing Type 2 DC SPDs at tower top and base; bonding 3-point grounding kits to external ground busbar; 19" rack installation of BBU; GPS antenna installation for PTP/SyncE; DC turn-up; software baseline loading; optical transceiver power verification; VSWR sweep testing (VSWR ≤ 1.35:1, Return Loss ≥ 16.5 dB); IP backhaul integration; Core (UPF/AMF) connection; OSS synchronization; alarm clearance; and commercial on-air declaration.

2.6 Single Site Verification (SSV) & Site Acceptance (ATP)

Mandatory three-stage acceptance procedure: ATP-A (Mechanical, Civil & Electrical inspection of bolt torque marks, cable dressing, weatherproofing, grounding resistance ≤ 5.0 Ohms); ATP-B (Active Telecom & Functional verification of 8T8R MU-MIMO operation, remote eRET tilt control, alarm failover simulations); and SSV (stationary throughput testing at near, mid, and cell-edge points per sector validating ≥ 450 Mbps peak DL, ≥ 50 Mbps cell-edge DL, ≥ 15 Mbps cell-edge UL, sector swap checks, handover tests, ping ≤ 18 ms RTT, and signed SSV Report).

2.7 Cluster Radio Network Optimization (RNO)

Multi-site cluster optimization across contiguous clusters of 10–20 sites: pre-launch scanner and test UE drive testing; electrical downtilt (eRET) optimization; azimuth adjustments; PCI re-planning; neighbor list cleanup; handover parameter tuning; and 14-day post-launch OSS statistical validation confirming cluster Radio Availability ≥ 99.95%, Session Setup Success Rate (CSSR) ≥ 99.60%, Session Drop Rate (CDDR) ≤ 0.15%, and cluster average throughput ≥ 450 Mbps.

3. OPERATIONS & MAINTENANCE (O&M) / MANAGED SERVICES SPECIFICATION

The Contractor shall deliver an integrated 24/7/365 Managed Services organization to ensure continuous 99.95% network availability across all 5,000 sites nationwide:

  • 24/7/365 Central NOC Surveillance: Tier-1 real-time surveillance across RAN, Core, Power, and Transmission via EMS/OSS. Automated alarm correlation, deduplication, and trouble ticket creation within 5 minutes. Daily Operational Flash Reports at 06:00 PHT, weekly reviews, and monthly SLA dossiers.
  • Tier-2 / Tier-3 Support: 24/7 remote diagnostics, soft/hard resets, card re-initialization, config restoration, and transmission failover. Tier-3 OEM TAC Level 3 escalation, bug patch testing, and Root Cause Analysis (RCA) reporting within 24 hours.
  • Field First Line Maintenance (FLM): Dedicated 24/7 mobile crews in 4x4 vehicles stationed at regional depots. Mandated MTTR: Severity 1 (Critical) ≤ 2.0h Urban / ≤ 4.0h Rural; Severity 2 (Major) ≤ 4.0h Urban / ≤ 6.0h Rural; Severity 3 (Minor) ≤ 12.0h Urban / ≤ 24.0h Rural. Includes active hardware swapping, passive electrical triage, and emergency fiber backhaul splicing.
  • Quarterly Preventive Maintenance (PM): 100% completion p.a. logged via Mobile App: Module A (Telecom Equipment & Optics); Module B (DC Power Plant & LiFePO4 Battery discharge test); Module C (Grounding grid ≤ 5.0 Ohms); Module D (Bi-annual tower climbing & rigging audit); Module E (Compound civil, fencing & weed clearance).
  • Spare Parts Management (SPMS): Regional depots maintaining an audited 2.0% active hardware float. Guaranteed Advance Hardware Replacement (AHR) Next-Business-Day (NBD) standard or 4-hour emergency delivery. Full RMA reverse logistics lifecycle tracking (TAT ≤ 30 days).
  • Continuous RNO & Interference Hunting: Ongoing RF KPI surveillance, weekly Worst Performing Cell (WPC) triage, spectrum analyzer sweeps for external jamming/boosters, and PIM testing (≤ -155 dBc).
  • Facility, Power & Software Maintenance: Tower anti-corrosion painting, plinth crack repair, utility Meralco/Coop coordination, 250-hour generator servicing, maintenance window patch deployments (00:00-05:00), and weekly automated backups.
