Dear Prospective Bidder,

Telecommunications Technology Solutions Incorporated (TelTech) (the **"Operator"** or **"Company"**), a duly franchised telecommunications entity operating pursuant to statutory authority and holding National Telecommunications Commission (NTC) Provisional Authority (**PA Case No. 2017-084** for Broadband Data Services and **PA Case No. 2017-291** for Nationwide Cellular Mobile Telephone System), formally invites your organization to participate in this competitive Request for Proposal (RFP).

The Operator has been officially granted a Temporary Permit to Propagate / Demonstrate (**Permit No. RB-09-01-2026/DEMO**, issued in reference to CPCN No. 2017-084) to conduct test broadcast, propagation, and technology demonstration in **Band n50 (1457 – 1517 MHz, 60 MHz channel bandwidth)**. Furthermore, the Operator holds an active, pending application for the permanent commercial assignment and operational deployment of the said frequency band pursuant to and subject to the provisions of the Open Access in Data Transmission Act (**Konektadong Pinoy Law**) and applicable NTC regulatory circulars.

### 1. Program Overview & Scope of Tender
This landmark national infrastructure initiative encompasses a planned footprint of **5,000 macro BTS sites** nationwide designed to serve over **2.45 to 2.66 Million active broadband subscriber households** across 5,000 prioritized barangays (2024 PSA POPCEN Official Census baseline):
* **Phase 1 Priority Rollout:** **1,000 Macro Sites** (Batch 1 — immediate rollout across prioritized unserved and underserved suburban and rural population centers).
* **Phases 2 to 5 Nationwide Expansion:** **1,000 Macro Sites per annum** over Years 2 through 5.
* **Turnkey Lots Available for Bidding:**
  * **LOT 1:** 5G Radio Access Network (RAN) Hardware, High-Gain Antennas & Turnkey Deployment Services
  * **LOT 2:** Power Systems & Passive Site Infrastructure (Co-location Interconnect & Hybrid DC Power)
  * **LOT 3:** Cloud-Native 5G Core Network (5GC SA) & Datacenter Infrastructure
  * **LOT 4:** Converged Digital OSS/BSS, Field Force Automation & Real-Time Charging Engine
  * **LOT 5:** RF Planning, Greenfield Site Survey, Civil Works & Cluster Optimization Services
  * **LOT 6:** Nationwide Managed Services, 24/7 Tier-2/Tier-3 NOC & Field Network Operations (MS)
  * **LOT 7:** 5G High-Gain Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) Terminals (Wi-Fi 6 AX1800)
  * **FORM D:** Mandatory Vendor Financing Facility (covering >= 80% of Capex with a minimum 12-month grace period)

### 2. Seeking Creative & Innovative Proposals
The Operator is actively seeking **creative, high-impact proposals—both technical and commercial**—from prospective bidders. We encourage prospective partners to propose innovative solutions that deliver:
1. **Industry-Leading Spectral Efficiency & Performance:** Advanced beamforming, optimal multi-user MIMO algorithms, and intelligent interference mitigation specifically optimized for Band n50 TDD topology.
2. **Innovative Commercial & Vendor Financing Frameworks:** Competitive financing mechanisms, attractive credit margins benchmarked against commercial indices, and vendor-backed milestone financing structures that conserve initial working capital.
3. **Rollout Velocity & Construction Innovation:** Rapid turnkey deployment workflows, standardized co-location installation kits for operational TowerCo infrastructure, and accelerated site integration models capable of executing 1,000 live sites in 12 months.
4. **Optimized 10-Year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO):** Low ongoing power consumption (high-efficiency rectifiers, intelligent energy-saving software), competitive long-term AMC rates, and automated managed operations.
