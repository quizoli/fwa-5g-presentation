**Dear Prospective Partner,**

**Subject: Request for Proposal (RFP) — Turnkey Supply, Commercial Deployment, Financing, and Managed Operations of a Nationwide 5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) Network**

Telecommunications Technology Solutions Incorporated (TelTech) (the **"Operator"** or **"Company"**) is pleased to officially invite your organization to submit binding technical and commercial proposals for the turnkey commercial rollout, infrastructure supply, financing, and end-to-end managed operations of our nationwide **5G Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) Network**.

This initiative targets the commercial deployment of **Band n50 (1432 – 1517 MHz, 1.5 GHz L-Band TDD)** with a **60 MHz contiguous carrier block**, engineered to provide high-speed, reliable, carrier-grade broadband across prioritized urban, suburban, and rural population centers in the Philippines.

### 1. Program Scale & Deployment Scope
The commercial rollout encompasses an aggregate footprint of **5,000 macro BTS sites** designed to serve over **2.45 to 2.66 Million active broadband subscriber households** across 5,000 barangays:
* **Phase 1 Commercial Launch:** **1,000 Macro Sites** (immediate priority deployment across high-demand unserved and underserved markets).
* **Phases 2 to 5 Nationwide Expansion:** **1,000 Macro Sites per annum** over Years 2 through 5.
* **Turnkey Lots Available for Bidding:**
  * **LOT 1:** 5G Radio Access Network (RAN) Hardware, Antennas & Turnkey Deployment Services
  * **LOT 2:** Power Systems & Passive Site Infrastructure (Co-location Interconnect & Hybrid DC Power)
  * **LOT 3:** Cloud-Native 5G Core Network (5GC SA) & Datacenter Infrastructure
  * **LOT 4:** Converged Digital OSS/BSS, Field Force Automation & Real-Time Charging Engine
  * **LOT 5:** RF Planning, Greenfield Site Survey, Civil Works & Cluster Optimization Services
  * **LOT 6:** Nationwide Managed Services, 24/7 Tier-2/Tier-3 NOC & Field Network Operations (MS)
  * **LOT 7:** 5G High-Gain Customer Premises Equipment (CPE) Terminals (Wi-Fi 6 AX1800)
  * **FORM D:** Vendor Financing Facility (covering >= 80% of Capex with milestone-based repayment terms)

### 2. Seeking Creative & High-Impact Proposals
TelTech is actively seeking **creative, forward-looking proposals—both technical and commercial**—that deliver competitive market advantages:
* **Spectral Efficiency & Performance:** Advanced Massive MIMO / beamforming algorithms tailored for Band n50 propagation to achieve maximum cell capacity and superior indoor penetration.
* **Innovative Financing Frameworks:** OEM vendor credit, export credit agency (ECA) backing, or structured milestone financing that optimizes capital outlay and conserves working capital during the initial buildout.
* **Rapid Turnkey Rollout Velocity:** Streamlined site acquisition, rapid co-location installation on operational TowerCo structures, and aggressive scheduling to achieve 1,000 on-air sites within 12 months.
* **Long-Term TCO Optimization:** Energy-efficient power and radio equipment, automated self-healing network operations, and highly competitive multi-year support agreements.
