2. KEY ARCHITECTURAL & CONTROLLING CLARIFICATIONS

2.1 Spectrum Physics, Band n50 Operating Bandwidth & Regional Boundaries

  1. Metro Manila (NCR) & Meralco Franchise Footprint:
  2. Operating Band: 1457 – 1517 MHz (60 MHz continuous channel bandwidth TDD).
  3. Resource Blocks: 162 RBs at 30 kHz Subcarrier Spacing (SCS).
  4. Boundary Condition: In Metro Manila (NCR) and non-NCR municipalities within the Meralco electric distribution franchise (parts of Bulacan, Cavite, Rizal, Laguna, Batangas, Quezon, and Pampanga) where the 1437–1457 MHz sub-band is assigned to the utility corporation, the 1437–1457 MHz block is excluded from Operator transmissions. RRU transceivers implement sharp digital filtering with Digital Pre-Distortion (DPD) to guarantee adjacent-channel isolation.
  5. Rest of the Country (Provincial / Regional / Non-Meralco Footprint):
  6. Operating Band: 1437 – 1517 MHz (up to 80 MHz continuous channel bandwidth TDD — Desired Baseline).
  7. Resource Blocks: 217 RBs at 30 kHz SCS (+34% capacity gain, unlocking 650–720 Mbps aggregate sector downlink throughput).
  8. Hardware Tuning Capability:
  9. All proposed Baseband Units (BBU) and Remote Radio Units (RRU) / Active Antenna Units (AAU) must natively support the full 3GPP Band n50 range (1432 – 1517 MHz) in hardware.
  10. Operating bandwidth must be software-configurable to 40 MHz, 50 MHz, 60 MHz, or 80 MHz without requiring tower-climbing or hardware replacement.
  11. Out-of-Band Rejection & Satellite Protection:
  12. High End (1517 MHz): Must comply with ITU-R M.2159-0 / WRC-15 Resolution 223 to protect adjacent Maritime Mobile Satellite Service (MSS, 1518 – 1525 MHz). Transceiver unwanted emissions in the 1518–1525 MHz band must not exceed -30 dBm/MHz at the antenna port. Standard 3GPP TS 38.104 Category B filtering is compliant.
  13. Low End (1437 / 1457 MHz): Must comply with 3GPP Category B Operating Band Unwanted Emissions (OBUE). Digital Pre-Distortion (DPD) within the RRU ensures sharp rolloff at the assigned carrier boundary.

2.2 Power Architecture & Rectifier Sizing Methodology (Lot 2)

  1. Installed Capacity: Sized at >= 12.0 kW total installed capacity using 4 × 3.0 kW (or 4 × 4.0 kW) modular hot-swappable switchmode rectifier modules in an N+1 architecture.
  2. Operational Load under N+1: With a single module failure (3 operational modules), the system provides 9.0 kW continuous DC output, which comfortably accommodates the maximum site DC load of 7.28 kW (3.0 kW BTS RAN load + 4.28 kW fast battery recharging at 0.2C for a 400 Ah / 20 kWh lithium bank).
  3. Mounting: Weather-resistant (IP55) integrated outdoor ground-mounted power/battery cabinets on an elevated concrete pad represent the baseline design. Pole-mounted or wall-mounted power units are approved for rooftop/space-constrained co-location sites.

2.3 Transmission, Backhaul Architecture & KPI Tiering

  1. Media-Tiered Latency SLAs (Document 04 / Appendix C Amendment):
    • Phase 1 Terrestrial Fiber-Connected Sites (100% of Phase 1): Ping Round-Trip Time (RTT) ≤ 18 ms target (≤ 30 ms maximum). Following executive fiber-priority rollout alignment, all 1,000 Phase 1 sites are assigned to terrestrial fiber backhaul (962 sites ≤ 1.0 km from existing fiber/nodes, and 38 sites > 1.0 km planned for lateral drops under Comclark optical rollout), eliminating satellite backhaul from Phase 1 scope.
    • Starlink Satellite-Fed Sites (Phase 2 / Expansion Batches): For remote/island candidates in subsequent rollout batches where terrestrial fiber is unviable, the LEO satellite latency threshold is relaxed to ≤ 60 ms target (≤ 80 ms maximum) during SSV and cluster acceptance to reflect satellite orbital physics.
  2. Backhaul Outage Exclusions:
    • Downtime, packet loss, or latency degradation caused by third-party fiber cuts, upstream IP transit failures, or satellite constellation outages outside the site compound are strictly excluded from supplier RAN availability SLA penalties.

2.4 TowerCo, Civil Works & Site Siting

  1. Phase 1 Siting Quantities & Commercial Allocation Baseline: The contractual commercial evaluated baseline across all RFP documents (Master Tender Section 2.1, BoQ Table 6, and Appendix D) is formally confirmed at 897 Co-location Sites (89.7%) and 103 Greenfield Monopole Sites (10.3%). In Document 07, spatial proximity screening against the audited master database classifies 893 Co-location Candidates (89.3%) within ≤ 2.0 km (551 Class T1 ≤ 0.5 km; 255 Class T2 0.5–1.0 km; 87 Class T3 1.0–2.0 km) and 107 Greenfield Monopole Candidates (10.7%) at Class T4 (> 2.0 km) nationwide. While tender evaluation and contract award remain strictly anchored on the 897 / 103 commercial baseline, actual site implementation counts will be determined during Milestone 1 TSSR approvals: if a screened greenfield site identifies an unmapped operational tower within the search ring, it converts to Tier 1 co-location; conversely, if an audited tower fails structural load verification, it converts to Tier 2 B2S or Tier 3 greenfield substitution.
  2. Commercial Valuation & Siting True-Up Mechanism: While tender evaluation and contract award are based on the lump-sum pricing of the 897 Co-Location / 103 Greenfield baseline, actual contractor compensation will be trued up at Milestone 1 (TSSR Approval). If an evaluated co-location site converts to Tier 2 B2S or Tier 3 Greenfield (or vice-versa), contract value adjustments shall be calculated strictly using Document 06 Table 5B (Siting Conversion & Milestone 1 True-Up Rate Schedule) via an administrative contract variation order:
    • Tier 1 Co-Location Baseline (LOT5-COLO): Rate = Item CIV-SVC-03 ($0.00 variation differential).
    • Tier 2 TowerCo Build-to-Suit (LOT5-B2S): Under the Operator MLA, the TowerCo finances, permits, and constructs the tower and compound turnkey; Operator civil scope is identical to co-location (CIV-SVC-03). Rate = LOT5-B2S = CIV-SVC-03. Variation differential vs. Co-Location = $0.00 / site (LOT5-B2S - LOT5-COLO = $0.00).
    • Tier 3 Greenfield 30m Monopole (LOT5-GF30): Compound foundation, 8x8m fence/gravel, grounding grid, 30m monopole, and SAQ permits (CIV-SVC-02 + CIV-SVC-04 + CIV-SVC-05 + CIV-SVC-06). Net contractual addition per converted co-location site: ΔCost = LOT5-GF30 - LOT5-COLO = (CIV-SVC-02 + CIV-SVC-04 + CIV-SVC-05 + CIV-SVC-06) - CIV-SVC-03. Reverse conversion from GF30 to Co-Lo issues an equivalent credit.
    • Tier 3 Greenfield 40m Monopole (LOT5-GF40): Compound foundation, 8x8m fence/gravel, grounding grid, 40m monopole, and SAQ permits (CIV-SVC-02 + CIV-SVC-04 + CIV-SVC-05 + CIV-SVC-07). Net contractual addition per converted co-location site: ΔCost = LOT5-GF40 - LOT5-COLO = (CIV-SVC-02 + CIV-SVC-04 + CIV-SVC-05 + CIV-SVC-07) - CIV-SVC-03. Reverse conversion from GF40 to Co-Lo issues an equivalent credit.
  3. Division of Structural Responsibilities (Audit vs. Analysis):
    • Contractor Scope (Structural Audit): The turnkey contractor's scope is strictly on-site physical auditing—recording tower member dimensions, mounting elevations, existing antenna counts, azimuths, and compiling the total proposed equipment loading profile (weights, surface areas, wind drag).
    • TowerCo / Operator Scope (Structural Analysis & Certification): Formal structural loading analysis, wind stress modeling, foundation capacity verification, and structural PE sign-off are performed by the TowerCo under the Operator's Master Lease Agreement (MLA). Required tower retrofits/reinforcements are executed by the TowerCo under the MLA.
  4. Site Hunting & Excluded Sites: Delays caused by local government (LGU) moratoria, right-of-way disputes, or utility connection delays exceeding 45 days permit the Operator and supplier to substitute the blocked candidate with a pre-approved site from the 4,000-candidate reserve pool without schedule penalty.
  5. Variation Orders (VOs): Abnormal site construction conditions (hard rock excavation requiring pneumatic equipment, manual material hauling > 500 meters across unpaved/mountainous terrain, and engineered retaining walls) are eligible for established unit-rate Variation Orders under Lot 5.
  6. Mandatory 3-Tier Infrastructure Sourcing Hierarchy (TowerCo-First Mandate): Siting must strictly follow:
    • Tier 1 (Mandatory Baseline): Co-location on existing operational towers across the Operator's audited master database of 13,810 nationwide TowerCo registry records (encompassing 13,799 unique Universal Tower IDs and 13,629 distinct geographic tower coordinates across PTCI/MIDC, EdgePoint, LDIC, FTAP Globe, and FTAP Smart). Reduces Ready-for-Installation (RFI) to 15–30 days.
    • Tier 2 (Secondary): TowerCo Build-to-Suit (B2S) under TelTech MLA if structural loading exceeds EPA limits on existing towers.
    • Tier 3 (Restricted Exception): Operator own-build greenfield monopoles are strictly restricted to search rings where no feasible or available TowerCo infrastructure exists after documented technical and field checks. Qualification operates strictly under two mutually exclusive pathways:
      • Pathway A (Zero Infrastructure / Siting Void): Confirmed total absence of operational towers within the primary search ring (500m urban / 1,000m rural) and within 2.0 km line-of-sight. Substantiation: Certified Absence of Tower Infrastructure in the approved joint TSSR, verified against the audited master database and 360° drone/visual survey.
      • Pathway B (Technical / Structural Exhaustion of Existing Infrastructure): Where operational towers exist within the search ring or within 2.0 km, but co-location is physically or structurally unfeasible due to: (i) structural over-utilization / EPA overload where the TowerCo confirms the tower cannot support additional 3x 8T8R AAU mounts and foundation reinforcement is unviable; (ii) physical compound/aperture exhaustion (zero ground space for outdoor DC power pad or no vertical mounting aperture at required AGL); or (iii) unresolvable terrain/foliage clutter blocking optical Fresnel zone line-of-sight into the target settlement cluster. Substantiation: Formal written Structural Denial / Space Unavailability Letter(s) issued by the operating TowerCo(s) under the MLA framework (a single denial letter if only 1 TowerCo operates in the ring; minimum 2 letters if multiple TowerCos operate within the ring), accompanied by TSSR RF simulation proving coverage blockage.
    • Survey Exception Candidates: Sited co-location candidate references located between 1.0 km and 2.0 km from the demand centroid (including 174 Phase 1 candidate references) are designated Survey Exception Candidates: during Milestone 1 TSSR, field teams evaluate whether clear line-of-sight and link budget (RSRP ≥ -105 dBm) can be achieved from the out-of-ring tower; if confirmed, the candidate is confirmed as Tier 1 Co-Location; if obstructed or unfeasible, the candidate converts under Pathway A or B to Tier 2 B2S or Tier 3 Greenfield at the nominal demand centroid.
  7. Search Ring Sizing & Mandatory 3-Candidate Rule (A, B, C):
    • Search Rings: Strictly 500 meters radius for Urban/Suburban; 1,000 meters radius for Rural/Remote centered on the nominal demand centroid. Search rings define site hunting limits and are completely distinct from RF cell coverage radii (2.5–3.2 km rural, 1.0–1.5 km urban).
    • 3-Candidate Presentation: Field teams must present Candidate A (BEST — Primary Target), Candidate B (BETTER — Secondary Backup), and Candidate C (GOOD — Fallback). Where fewer than three viable structures exist in remote rural rings, documented justification in the TSSR is permitted.
    • Commercial Authority: All commercial lease negotiations, space reservations, and MLA Service Orders with TowerCos are strictly the exclusive responsibility of the Operator (TelTech/Comclark). The contractor's scope is technical identification, structural audit data gathering, and TSSR submission.
    • 14-Day Technical Escalation Protocol: If Candidate A fails structural loading verification or if Operator-TowerCo commercial SAA processing encounters an impasse exceeding 14 calendar days, the Operator shall instruct the contractor to immediately activate Candidate B to prevent project delay.
  8. Structural Wind Load Standards & Deflection Limits (NSCP 2015 7th Ed. / TIA-222-H):
    • Governing Design Standard: National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP 2015 7th Edition, Section 207 Wind Loads) and ANSI/TIA-222-H. Structure classification: Essential Communications Facility / Risk Category IV (Importance Factor Iw = 1.15).
    • Nominal Basic Design Wind Speed (ASD): Minimum basic design wind speed is 250 km/h (ASD nominal fastest-mile / 10-minute mean equivalent per NSCP 2015 Category IV) for structural strength and foundation stability calculations.
    • Ultimate / Survivable Wind Speed (Vult / LRFD 3-second gust): Conforms strictly to NSCP 2015 Wind Zones:
      • Zone 1 (Vult = 315 km/h / 87.5 m/s): High-typhoon eastern seaboard (Batanes, Cagayan, Isabela, Aurora, eastern Quezon coast/Polillo, Bicol Region, Samar, Leyte, Dinagat Islands, Surigao del Norte/Sur, Davao Oriental).
      • Zone 2 (Vult = 270 km/h / 75.0 m/s): Central and Western Luzon (NCR, Bulacan, Pampanga, Tarlac, Bataan, Zambales, Nueva Ecija), CALABARZON (western Quezon, Laguna, Batangas, Cavite, Rizal), MIMAROPA (Occidental/Oriental Mindoro, Marinduque, Romblon), and Western/Central Visayas (Iloilo, Cebu, Bohol, Negros).
      • Zone 3 (Vult = 250 km/h / 69.4 m/s Minimum Project Floor): Low-risk regions (Palawan, Zamboanga Peninsula, and southern inland Mindanao). While NSCP maps Zone 3 at 200 km/h, the Project enforces 250 km/h minimum ultimate survivability nationwide as a mandatory contractual quality floor.
    • Operational Wind Speed: 140 – 160 km/h (Serviceability Limit State per TIA-222-H).
    • Allowable Twist: ≤ ±0.5° for MW, ≤ ±1.0° for macro sector; Allowable Sway: ≤ ±1.0°; Verticality (Plumbness): ≤ 1:500 (0.20% of height H = 60 mm for 30m tower). Details codified in revised Appendix D.
  9. Panoramic Drone Survey & Permitting Waiver: High-resolution 360° panoramic video and cardinal-point photos at antenna centerline. Conditional Waiver: Where drone flight is prohibited/restricted by CAAP or military airspace regulations, OR where local municipal/flight permits cannot be secured within fourteen (14) calendar days, high-magnification telephoto mast photography from multiple ground vantage points combined with verified TowerCo structural drawings is acceptable upon Operator approval.

2.5 Digital OSS/BSS & 5G Core Architecture

  1. Turnkey BSS Scope: Real-time Prepaid Convergent Charging System (CCS/CHF via 3GPP Nchf/Diameter Ro) for the 4 consumer FWA tiers (₱599, ₱799, ₱999, ₱1,299). Complex cyclic enterprise invoicing is handled via integration with Comclark/TelTech's existing enterprise billing platform.
  2. SIM-IMEI & Cell-ID Location Locking: The BSS/PCF must enforce strict hardware-network binding to ensure FWA service cannot be operated outside the authorized installation address.
  3. Core Network Functions (NEF & SEPP): In Phase 1, NEF (Network Exposure) and SEPP (Security Edge Protection Proxy) are optional/phased roadmap functions.
  4. Compute Platform: Dual-socket carrier-grade x86 (Intel/AMD) or equivalent enterprise ARM-based architecture running certified carrier-grade Linux / Kubernetes is fully acceptable.

2.6 Customer Premises Equipment (CPE - Lot 7)

  1. Operating Band: Native Band n50 (1432 – 1517 MHz, 60 MHz TDD) in 5G SA mode is mandatory.
  2. Indoor CPE Antenna Gain: Integrated antenna array gain of 5.0 – 6.0 dBi across Band n50 is accepted as compliant (leveraging the superior propagation of 1.5 GHz compared to 3.5 GHz). Outdoor CPE requires >= 12.0 dBi.
  3. SIM Form Factor: Physical Nano-SIM (4FF) with anti-theft locking cover is mandatory. eSIM (MFF2) is an acceptable optional enhancement.
