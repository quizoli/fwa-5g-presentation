2. KEY ARCHITECTURAL & CONTROLLING CLARIFICATIONS

2.1 Spectrum Physics & Operating Frequency (Band n50)

  1. Metro Manila (NCR) Spectrum Deployment:
  2. Operating Band: 1457 – 1517 MHz (60 MHz continuous channel bandwidth TDD).
  3. The 1437 – 1457 MHz sub-band in NCR is assigned to another entity and will not be utilized by the Operator in NCR.
  4. Rest of the Country (Provincial / Regional Deployment):
  5. Operating Band: 1437 – 1517 MHz (up to 80 MHz continuous channel bandwidth TDD).
  6. Hardware Tuning Capability:
  7. All proposed Baseband Units (BBU) and Remote Radio Units (RRU) / Active Antenna Units (AAU) must natively support the full 3GPP Band n50 range (1432 – 1517 MHz) in hardware.
  8. Operating bandwidth must be software-configurable to 40 MHz, 50 MHz, 60 MHz, or 80 MHz without requiring tower-climbing or hardware replacement.
  9. Out-of-Band Rejection & Satellite Protection:
  10. High End (1517 MHz): Must comply with ITU-R M.2159-0 / WRC-15 Resolution 223 to protect adjacent Maritime Mobile Satellite Service (MSS, 1518 – 1525 MHz). Transceiver unwanted emissions in the 1518–1525 MHz band must not exceed -30 dBm/MHz at the antenna port. Standard 3GPP TS 38.104 Category B filtering is compliant.
  11. Low End (1437 / 1457 MHz): Must comply with 3GPP Category B Operating Band Unwanted Emissions (OBUE). Digital Pre-Distortion (DPD) within the RRU ensures sharp rolloff at the assigned carrier boundary.

2.2 Power Architecture & Rectifier Sizing Methodology (Lot 2)

  1. Installed Capacity: Sized at >= 12.0 kW total installed capacity using 4 × 3.0 kW (or 4 × 4.0 kW) modular hot-swappable switchmode rectifier modules in an N+1 architecture.
  2. Operational Load under N+1: With a single module failure (3 operational modules), the system provides 9.0 kW continuous DC output, which comfortably accommodates the maximum site DC load of 7.28 kW (3.0 kW BTS RAN load + 4.28 kW fast battery recharging at 0.2C for a 400 Ah / 20 kWh lithium bank).
  3. Mounting: Weather-resistant (IP55) integrated outdoor ground-mounted power/battery cabinets on an elevated concrete pad represent the baseline design. Pole-mounted or wall-mounted power units are approved for rooftop/space-constrained co-location sites.

2.3 Transmission, Starlink Satellite Backhaul & KPI Tiering

  1. Media-Tiered Latency SLAs (Document 04 / Appendix C Amendment):
  2. Terrestrial Fiber-Fed Sites (98.3% of Phase 1): Ping Round-Trip Time (RTT) <= 18 ms target (<= 30 ms maximum).
  3. Starlink Satellite-Fed Sites (1.7% / 17 island/remote sites): Ping RTT threshold is relaxed to <= 60 ms target (<= 80 ms maximum) during SSV and cluster acceptance to reflect LEO satellite orbital physics.
  4. Backhaul Outage Exclusions:
  5. Downtime, packet loss, or latency degradation caused by third-party fiber cuts, upstream IP transit failures, or satellite constellation outages outside the site compound are strictly excluded from vendor RAN availability SLA penalties.

2.4 TowerCo, Civil Works & Site Siting

  1. Tower Structural Loading Verification: For the 897 Co-location sites, structural loading analysis is conducted by the TowerCo under the Operator's Master Lease Agreement (MLA). The vendor provides equipment weights, dimensions, wind load surface area, and cable routing. Any necessary tower retrofits are executed by the TowerCo under the MLA.
  2. Site Hunting & Excluded Sites: Delays caused by local government (LGU) moratoria, right-of-way disputes, or utility connection delays exceeding 45 days permit the Operator and vendor to substitute the blocked candidate with a pre-approved site from the 4,000-candidate reserve pool without schedule penalty.
  3. Variation Orders (VOs): Abnormal site construction conditions (hard rock excavation requiring pneumatic equipment, manual material hauling > 500 meters across unpaved/mountainous terrain, and engineered retaining walls) are eligible for established unit-rate Variation Orders under Lot 5.

2.5 Digital OSS/BSS & 5G Core Architecture

  1. Turnkey BSS Scope: Real-time Prepaid Convergent Charging System (CCS/CHF via 3GPP Nchf/Diameter Ro) for the 4 consumer FWA tiers (₱599, ₱799, ₱999, ₱1,299). Complex cyclic enterprise invoicing is handled via integration with Converge's existing enterprise billing platform.
  2. SIM-IMEI & Cell-ID Location Locking: The BSS/PCF must enforce strict hardware-network binding to ensure FWA service cannot be operated outside the authorized installation address.
  3. Core Network Functions (NEF & SEPP): In Phase 1, NEF (Network Exposure) and SEPP (Security Edge Protection Proxy) are optional/phased roadmap functions.
  4. Compute Platform: Dual-socket carrier-grade x86 (Intel/AMD) or equivalent ARM-based compute (e.g. Huawei Kunpeng) running certified carrier-grade Linux / Kubernetes is fully acceptable.

2.6 Customer Premises Equipment (CPE - Lot 7)

  1. Operating Band: Native Band n50 (1432 – 1517 MHz, 60 MHz TDD) in 5G SA mode is mandatory.
  2. Indoor CPE Antenna Gain: Integrated antenna array gain of 5.0 – 6.0 dBi across Band n50 is accepted as compliant (leveraging the superior propagation of 1.5 GHz compared to 3.5 GHz). Outdoor CPE requires >= 12.0 dBi.
  3. SIM Form Factor: Physical Nano-SIM (4FF) with anti-theft locking cover is mandatory. eSIM (MFF2) is an acceptable optional enhancement.
