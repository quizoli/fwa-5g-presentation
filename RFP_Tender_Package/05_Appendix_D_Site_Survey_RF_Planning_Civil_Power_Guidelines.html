1. ENGINEERING PRINCIPLES & SITING ARCHITECTURE

1.1 Siting Philosophy

The primary engineering objective of the 5G FWA rollout is to maximize household population coverage within high-density suburban and rural barangay settlement clusters while minimizing civil capital expenditures and permitting lead times.

  • TowerCo Co-Location First (Option A — Baseline): The Operator leverages an audited master database of 13,746 operational towers. For every candidate barangay, field teams must prioritize existing towers within $\le 2.0\text{ km}$ (Classes T1, T2, and T3).
  • Greenfield Monopole (Class T4): Restricted strictly to candidate barangays located $> 2.0\text{ km}$ from any verified operational tower structure (~9.3% of sites nationwide; 103 sites in Phase 1).

2. DETAILED TECHNICAL SITE SURVEY REPORT (TSSR) REQUIREMENTS

For every nominal site candidate, the Contractor shall complete and submit a comprehensive TSSR conforming to the following structure:

2.1 General Site Identification & Geography

  • Site Name, Candidate ID, Official PSGC Code, Region, Province, Municipality, and Barangay.
  • Precision WGS84 GPS Coordinates (Latitude, Longitude recorded with $\ge 6$ decimal places via dual-frequency GPS receiver).
  • Ground Elevation Above Mean Sea Level (AMSL in meters).
  • Access Road Assessment: Road surface type (concrete, asphalt, macadam, dirt trail), bridge weight limits, and 4x4 hauling accessibility during monsoon seasons.

2.2 Tower Structure & Loading Audit (Co-Location Sites)

  • Operating TowerCo Entity (PTCI/MIDC, FTAP Globe, EdgePoint, FTAP Smart, LDIC, FTAP BTS).
  • Tower Type (Self-Supporting 3-Leg/4-Leg Tower, Monopole, Guyed Mast, Rooftop Tower).
  • Tower Height Above Ground Level (AGL in meters).
  • Structural Space Audit: Proposed mounting height for 3x Band n50 antennas (recommended centerline: 30m to 45m AGL).
  • Structural Loading Verification: Confirmation that existing tower members and foundation can accommodate additional 3x 8T8R RRUs/AAUs ($<45\text{ kg}$ each) and wind load profile of $250\text{ km/h}$.
  • Panoramic Drone Photographic Survey: High-resolution $360^\circ$ continuous panoramic video and 12-cardinal-point still photos at proposed antenna centerline height to verify optical line-of-sight across populated settlements.

2.3 Power Infrastructure & Utility Audit

  • Existing Commercial AC Utility Power: Electric Cooperative / Distribution Utility name (e.g., MERALCO, PELCO, BENECO, CAPELCO, ILPI).
  • Service Entrance Capacity: Transformer rating (kVA), service voltage (230V / 400V, 60 Hz), breaker rating, and phase balance.
  • TowerCo DC Power Availability: Sourcing from TowerCo DC busbar (-48V DC) vs. installing Operator-dedicated rectifier subrack.
  • Sub-metering feasibility and cable tray routing for power cables.
  • Grounding System Audit: Measurement of existing earth ground resistance using a 3-point fall-of-potential earth tester. Earth resistance must be $\le 5.0\text{ Ohms}$.

2.4 Backhaul Transmission Path Survey

  • Audited Codex Optical Screening Match: Distance to nearest confirmed node candidate (Class A $\le 0.5\text{ km}$, Class B $\le 1.0\text{ km}$) or line-tap closure (Class C $\le 0.5\text{ km}$).
  • Cable Entry Facility: Manhole, handhole, or aerial pole lead-in routing into tower shelter/compound.
  • For Remote Class E/F Sites: Satellite antenna mounting location (unobstructed sky view with $0^\circ$ to $80^\circ$ northern elevation arc for Starlink constellation tracking).

3.2 Nominal Sector Azimuth & Tilt Strategy

  • Sector Count: 3 sectors per macro site with $120^\circ$ nominal separation ($0^\circ, 120^\circ, 240^\circ$). Azimuths must be tailored during TSSR to track actual barangay population concentrations along transport corridors and coastal plains.
  • Electrical Remote Downtilt (eRET): Antennas must feature AISG 2.0 remote tilt adjustable between $2^\circ$ and $10^\circ$ from the central OSS. Mechanical tilt shall only be applied when required to clear close-in obstructions ($>15^\circ$).

4. CIVIL WORKS & STRUCTURAL ENGINEERING (CLASS T4 GREENFIELD SITES)

For the ~10% of sites requiring self-constructed greenfield towers, the Contractor shall adhere to the following civil standards:

  1. Monopole Tower Specification:
  • 30-meter or 40-meter polygonal tubular steel monopole constructed in 3 to 4 slip-joint sections.
  • Structural steel: ASTM A572 Grade 50 (or equivalent high-strength structural grade) hot-dip galvanized per ASTM A123 ($\ge 85\text{ microns}$ zinc coating).
  • Design Wind Speed: Minimum $250\text{ km/h}$ 3-second gust in accordance with the National Structural Code of the Philippines (NSCP 2015 / TIA-222-G/H).
  • Top antenna mounting platform capable of supporting 6x antennas/RRUs and microwave dish mountings.
  1. Compound Civil Works:
  • Compound Footprint: Standard $8.0\text{m} \times 8.0\text{m}$ (64 m²) gravel-topped compound.
  • Perimeter Fencing: 2.4-meter high cyclone wire mesh fence topped with 3 strands of galvanized concertina razor wire.
  • Equipment Pad: Reinforced concrete equipment plinth (Class A concrete, 28-day compressive strength $\ge 21\text{ MPa} / 3,000\text{ psi}$) for outdoor cabinets and generator set.
  1. Lightning Protection & Grounding Grid:
  • Air terminal (lightning rod) installed at monopole top extending $\ge 2.0\text{m}$ above uppermost antenna.
  • Down-conductor: $70\text{ mm²}$ bare stranded copper cable routed externally and clamped every 1.0m to tower shaft.
  • Earth ground ring: Continuous buried bare copper loop around tower base and compound with copper-clad steel ground rods ($19\text{mm dia} \times 3.0\text{m}$ length) exothermically welded (Cadweld) at all junctions, achieving $\le 5.0\text{ Ohms}$.

5. SINGLE SITE VERIFICATION (SSV) & CLUSTER ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

Before any site is granted Commercial On-Air Acceptance, the Contractor must complete and submit a signed SSV Report verifying:

  1. Stationary Functional Tests:
  • Sector Verification: Physical azimuth and tilt match TSSR approval within $\pm 2^\circ$.
  • RF Transmit Power: Conducted power on all 8 transmit paths within $\pm 0.5\text{ dB}$ of nominal.
  • VSWR / Return Loss: Return loss $\ge 15\text{ dB}$ across all antenna feeder lines.
  • Max Throughput Test: Single CPE situated in line-of-sight achieving $\ge 450\text{ Mbps}$ DL and $\ge 60\text{ Mbps}$ UL.
  1. Short Drive Test & Static Multi-Point Test:
  • 5 stationary measurement points per sector (Near: 500m, Mid: 1.5km, Far/Edge: 3.0km).
  • RSRP $\ge -95\text{ dBm}$ across $\ge 90\%$ of surveyed barangay inhabited zone.
  • Ping RTT $\le 20\text{ ms}$ to local UPF IP address; zero packet loss over 1,000 continuous pings.
  • Inter-sector handover execution without drop during transition.
