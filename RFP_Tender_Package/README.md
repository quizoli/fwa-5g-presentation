## 2. Summary of Tender Scope by Lot

Bidders may submit proposals for **Turnkey (All Lots)** or for individual standalone lots. Standalone lot submissions must include all mandatory items specified within that lot:

* **LOT 1 — 5G Radio Access Network (RAN) Hardware & Telecom Implementation:**  
  Supply, installation, commissioning, and single site verification (SSV) of carrier-grade 3-sector 8T8R Massive MIMO gNodeB equipment operating across Band n50 (1432–1517 MHz, 60 MHz channel bandwidth) with 320W total RF output per sector.
* **LOT 2 — Power Systems & Passive Site Infrastructure:**  
  Modular 6.0 kW switchmode DC power plants (N+1), Lithium Iron Phosphate (LiFePO4) battery energy storage systems delivering $\ge 12.0\text{ kWh}$ usable energy over 4.0 hours @ 3.0 kW load ($\ge 19.0$–$20.0\text{ kWh}$ installed nominal bank capacity), TowerCo tap-off boxes, and 15 kVA diesel standby generators.
* **LOT 3 — Cloud-Native 5G Core Network (5GC SA) & Datacenter Infrastructure:**  
  Active-Active dual-datacenter cloud-native 5G Standalone core network (Metro Manila Primary DC and Clark DR DC) dimensioned for 1.0M initial registered subscribers with wire-speed DPDK UPF nodes and carrier-grade CG-NAT.
* **LOT 4 — Converged Digital OSS/BSS & Field Force Automation:**  
  Cloud-native converged charging (CHF), prepaid rating engine, real-time Philippine digital payment integration (GCash, Maya), mobile field-force dispatch application (3,500 daily work orders), and warehouse logistics.
* **LOT 5 — Site Acquisition, Detailed Site Survey (TSSR), RF Design & Civil Works:**  
  Technical Site Survey Reports (TSSR), LGU and barangay permitting, reinforced concrete equipment pads, grounding rings ($\le 5.0\ \Omega$), 30m/40m monopole tower supply and erection, and cluster RF optimization.
* **LOT 6 — Nationwide Managed Services & 24/7 Network Operations (MS):**  
  24/7/365 Tier-2/Tier-3 central NOC monitoring, nationwide First Line Maintenance (FLM), quarterly preventive maintenance, and Next-Business-Day (NBD) depot spare parts logistics.
* **LOT 7 — 5G High-Gain Customer Premises Equipment (CPE):**  
  350,000 Indoor 5G FWA Routers ($\ge 8.0\text{ dBi}$ internal antenna, Wi-Fi 6 AX1800/AX3000, 2.5GE LAN) and 15,000 Outdoor 5G Terminals ($\ge 12.0\text{ dBi}$ directional antenna, IP67, 2.5GE PoE with companion indoor Wi-Fi 6 router), plus custom firmware staging for all 365,000 terminals.
* **FORM D — Mandatory Vendor Financing Facility:**  
  Vendor-backed financing facility covering at least 80% of Capex, minimum 4-year tenor (5 years preferred), and a minimum 12-month grace period on principal repayment.

## 3. Bidder Submission Envelope Structure

Bidders must organize their formal response strictly into three (3) separately bound and sealed volumes:

* **Volume I: Commercial Proposal & Vendor Financing (Financial Envelope):**
  * Form A: Bid Submission Letter and Joint Venture / Consortium Agreement (if applicable).
  * Form B: Completed Price Book (`06_RFP_Commercial_Price_Book_and_BoQ_Template.xlsx`) with firm, binding unit rates in USD and PHP.
  * Form C: Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) breakdown covering Day-1 Capex, expansion milestones, and 10-year Opex.
  * Form D: Binding Vendor Financing Term Sheet signed by an authorized financial officer.
* **Volume II: Technical Proposal & Compliance Matrices (Technical Envelope):**
  * Section A to Section F: Detailed engineering design, system architectures, network topology diagrams, and implementation plans.
  * Appendix A Compliance Matrix: Line-by-line statement of compliance (**Compliant / Minor Deviation / Non-Compliant**) against every technical requirement.
  * Original equipment manufacturer (OEM) datasheets, type approval certificates, and test reports.
* **Volume III: Bidder Qualifications, References & Legal Documents:**
  * Proof of at least three (3) commercial 5G deployments of comparable scale within the last 36 months.
  * Audited financial balance sheets and income statements for the past three (3) fiscal years.
  * SEC registration, local Philippine branch/subsidiary documentation, and local support entity profile.
